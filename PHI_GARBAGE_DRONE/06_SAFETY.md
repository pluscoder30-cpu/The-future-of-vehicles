## MATERIALS NOT TO COLLECT

| Material | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Broken glass | Sharp, can damage gripper |
| Needles/syringes | Biohazard |
| Chemicals | Hazardous |
| Batteries | Can leak/harmful |
| Radioactive material | Dangerous |
| Large items | Too heavy for drone |
