# PHI GARBAGE DRONE — Kid-Friendly Instruction Manual

## What Is the PHI Garbage Drone?

The PHI Garbage Drone is a flying robot that picks up trash! It has a robotic arm that can grab garbage, and it sorts the trash into different bins for recycling. It costs less than $320 to build!

### How Does It Pick Up Trash?

1. **FINDS** — Camera and sensors find trash on the ground
2. **GRABS** — Robotic arm picks up the trash
3. **SORTS** — Puts trash in the right recycling bin
