# PHI GARBAGE DRONE — SORTING PROTOCOLS

## Garbage Collection and Sorting Guidelines

---

## COLLECTION PROCEDURE

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Fly over littered area |
| 2 | Scan with camera for trash |
| 3 | Detect trash with IR sensors |
| 4 | Approach trash item |
| 5 | Activate robotic arm |
| 6 | Pick up trash with gripper |
| 7 | Identify material (metal/IR/camera) |
| 8 | Sort into correct bin |
| 9 | Continue until bins full |

---

## SORTING DECISION TREE

```
SORTING LOGIC:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Trash detected
       │
       ▼
  Metal detector triggered?
       │
  YES ─┤─ NO
  │         │
  ▼         ▼
  METAL    IR reflection strong?
  BIN           │
           YES ─┤─ NO
           │         │
           ▼         ▼
         PLASTIC   Camera identifies
         BIN       │
                   ▼
                 PAPER or ORGANIC
                 BIN (if paper)
                 DISCARD (if organic)
```

