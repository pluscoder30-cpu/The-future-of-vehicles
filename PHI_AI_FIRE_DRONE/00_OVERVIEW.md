## WHAT IS THE PHI AI FIRE DRONE?

The PHI AI Fire Drone is an AI-enhanced fire detection and suppression drone that predicts fire spread, coordinates drone swarms, and optimizes retardant drops. It patrols forested areas, detects fires early using thermal imaging, and coordinates suppression efforts with other drones.

The drone measures 500mm x 500mm x 300mm, weighs 3.0 kg, and carries up to 2.0 kg of fire retardant. It is powered by FPB-10 (24V, 50Ah) battery system providing 3 hours of continuous operation.

## AI CAPABILITIES

### 1. Fire Spread Prediction

AI models predict how fire will spread based on:
- Wind speed and direction
- Terrain slope and elevation
- Fuel type and moisture content
- Current fire size and intensity

### 2. Drone Swarm Coordination

AI coordinates multiple fire drones:
- Assigns sectors to each drone
- Manages retardant allocation
- Prevents overlap and gaps
- Real-time reassignment as conditions change

### 3. Retardant Drop Optimization

AI calculates optimal drops:
- Trajectory based on wind and altitude
- Timing for maximum effectiveness
- Pattern selection (line, spot, surround)
- Retardant amount per drop
