## WHAT IS THE PHI AI FIRE DRONE?

The PHI AI Fire Drone is a flying robot with AI that fights fires! It uses a thermal camera to find fires, AI to predict where they'll spread, and drops retardant to stop them. It can work with other drones to fight big fires together!

### How Does It Fight Fires?

The drone has FOUR ways to help:

1. **FINDS FIRES** — Thermal camera sees heat through smoke
2. **PREDICTS SPREAD** — AI figures out where fire will go
3. **DROPS RETARDANT** — Sprays fire-stopping liquid
4. **WORKS WITH OTHERS** — AI coordinates drone swarm

## How to Use the AI Features

### Starting a Fire Patrol

```
AI FIRE PATROL:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Fill retardant tank (2 liters)
  2. Charge batteries fully
  3. Turn on drone
  4. Wait for AI to boot (blue LED)
  5. Connect phone to drone WiFi
  6. Open drone app
  7. Press "AI PATROL"
  8. Drone flies patrol route
  9. AI watches for fires
  10. If fire found → AI alerts you!
```
