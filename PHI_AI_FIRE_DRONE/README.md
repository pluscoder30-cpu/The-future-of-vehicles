## WHAT IS THIS?

The PHI AI Fire Drone is a dirt-cheap fire fighting drone with AI capabilities you can build for under $450. It predicts how fires will spread, coordinates with other drones to fight fires together, and optimizes where to drop retardant.

**It is not a toy. It is a real, functional AI-assisted fire suppression drone.**

## AI FEATURES

### Fire Spread Prediction
- AI models fire behavior based on wind, terrain, and fuel
- Predicts fire spread direction and rate
- Calculates optimal drop zones

### Drone Swarm Coordination
- Coordinates multiple fire drones
- Assigns sectors to prevent overlap
- Real-time reassignment as conditions change

### Retardant Drop Optimization
- Calculates optimal trajectory
- Adjusts for wind and altitude
- Selects best drop pattern
