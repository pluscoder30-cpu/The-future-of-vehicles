## FIRST TIME SETUP

### Step 1: Charge the Watch
1. Place watch on Qi charging pad
2. LED will blink blue (charging)
3. Wait until LED is solid green (fully charged)
4. **Time**: 45 minutes for full charge

### Step 2: Power On
1. Watch powers on automatically when removed from charger
2. You'll see the PHI logo
3. Wait for setup screen

### Step 3: Pair with Phone
1. Download "PHI Health" app:
   - iOS: App Store → search "PHI Health"
   - Android: Play Store → search "PHI Health"
2. Open app and tap "Add Device"
3. Watch will appear as "PHI-BIO-WATCH"
4. Tap to pair
5. Enter pairing code: **123456**

### Step 4: Set Up Profile
1. Enter your name
2. Set birthday
3. Enter height and weight
4. Select dominant hand
5. Accept terms of service

### Step 5: Calibrate Sensors
1. Wear watch on non-dominant wrist
2. Hold still for 30 seconds
3. Follow on-screen prompts
4. Calibration complete!

## SELF-CLEANING FEATURE

### How It Works
The watch has a special surface that automatically cleans itself:
- **Contact angle**: 168° (superhydrophobic)
- **Self-cleaning time**: 30 seconds
- **Cleaning effectiveness**: 98.3%

### What It Removes
- Fingerprints
- Oil smudges
- Dust particles
- Water spots
- Bacteria

### When It Cleans
The surface cleans itself:
- When exposed to water
- When exposed to humidity
- When tilted at an angle
- Automatically throughout the day

### To Manually Clean
1. Drop water on display
2. Tilt watch to let water roll off
3. Surface is now clean!

### Maintenance
The self-cleaning surface lasts:
- **Normal use**: 37.6 hours between manual cleaning
- **Heavy use**: Reapply fluoropolymer every 6 months
- **Band**: Clean with soap and water weekly

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Watch Won't Turn On
1. Place on charger for 5 minutes
2. Remove from charger
3. If still off, hold power button 10 seconds
4. If still off, contact support

### Display Blank
1. Tap display to wake
2. Swipe to check all screens
3. Restart watch (hold power 10 seconds)
4. If still blank, contact support

### Sensors Not Working
1. Ensure proper wearing position
2. Clean sensor area
3. Restart watch
4. Recalibrate (Settings → Sensors → Calibrate)
5. If still not working, contact support

### BLE Won't Connect
1. Ensure Bluetooth is on (phone)
2. Forget device in Bluetooth settings
3. Re-pair from PHI Health app
4. Restart both watch and phone
5. If still not working, contact support

### Self-Cleaning Not Working
1. Clean display with water
2. Apply fluoropolymer (see maintenance)
3. If still not working, contact support
