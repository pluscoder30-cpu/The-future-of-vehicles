# 10 — STASIS SYSTEMS

## Overview

The conscious stasis system requires six supporting subsystems:

1. **Power System** — FPB batteries and phi-harmonic field power
2. **Monitoring System** — Vital signs and consciousness coherence
3. **Life Support** — Air, water, nutrition, waste
4. **Awakening System** — Gradual frequency release
5. **Emergency Awakening** — Rapid field termination
6. **Communication** — Consciousness-to-consciousness field linking

Each subsystem is designed for the specific requirements of conscious stasis — maintaining a phi-harmonic standing wave that preserves consciousness while the body is at minimal metabolic activity.
