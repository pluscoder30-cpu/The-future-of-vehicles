- **Lumber Yard:** Sitka spruce, pine boards, balsa (cheapest structural source)
- **Home Depot:** Hardware, fasteners, plywood, tools, epoxy, sandpaper
- **Amazon:** Electronics, sensors, safety gear, specialty items
- **AliExpress:** Brushless motors, ESCs, sensors, propeller components
- **eBay Surplus:** Dacron fabric, aircraft hardware, radio equipment
- **Aircraft Spruce (aircraftspruce.com):** AN bolts, nuts, washers (aviation-grade)
- **Hobby Shop:** Balsa wood, tissue, model aircraft supplies
