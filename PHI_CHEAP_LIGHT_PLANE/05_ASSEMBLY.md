# PHI CHEAP LIGHT PLANE — ASSEMBLY

## Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions

---

## ASSEMBLY OVERVIEW

**Total Build Time:** 200-300 hours (1-2 builders, 2-4 months)
**Skill Level:** Intermediate Maker / Woodworking / Basic Electronics
**Tools Required:** Drill, circular saw, hand saw, sandpaper, clamps, soldering iron
**Workspace:** Single-car garage or equivalent (3m × 6m minimum)

### Build Phases

| Phase | Description | Hours | Dependency |
|-------|-------------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Frame Fabrication | 60-80 | None |
| 2 | Wing Construction | 40-60 | Phase 1 |
| 3 | Tail Surfaces | 15-20 | Phase 1 |
| 4 | Landing Gear | 10-15 | Phase 1 |
| 5 | Fabric Covering | 30-40 | Phase 1-3 |
| 6 | Motor & Propulsion | 15-20 | Phase 1 |
| 7 | Electrical System | 20-30 | Phase 1, 6 |
| 8 | Avionics | 15-20 | Phase 7 |
| 9 | Final Assembly | 20-30 | All above |
| 10 | Testing & Inspection | 10-15 | Phase 9 |
| **TOTAL** | | **235-330** | |

## PHASE 2: WING CONSTRUCTION

### Step 2.1: Cut Wing Spar

```
WING SPAR PREPARATION:
──────────────────────

Materials needed:
- 1×4" Sitka Spruce × 8ft: 4 boards (2 per wing)

Each spar is 5m long, made from 2× 8ft boards spliced.

SPAR SPLICE:
- Overlap: 400mm (longer than fuselage splices for strength)
- Bolts: 6× AN3 bolts per splice
- Gussets: 2× 1/4" plywood, 150mm × 150mm

SPAR NOTCHES (for ribs):
- Cut 12.5mm wide × 25mm deep notches every 400mm
- Notch position: centered on spar (44.5mm from edge)
- Total notches per spar: 13
```

### Step 2.2: Cut Wing Ribs

```
RIB FABRICATION:
────────────────

Materials needed:
- 1/2"×1" Spruce strips × 8ft: 20 boards
- 1"×1" Balsa blocks × 12": 12 blocks
- 1/4"×1/4" Pine strips × 4ft: 30 strips

RIB CUTTING:
1. Create Clark Y template on 1/4" plywood
2. Trace template onto 1/2"×1" spruce strips
3. Cut ribs with coping saw or band saw
4. Sand smooth with 120-grit sandpaper
5. Cut spar notch at 30% chord (89mm from LE)
6. Cut trailing edge notch at 100% chord

RIB QUANTITY:
- Left wing: 13 ribs
- Right wing: 13 ribs
- Total: 26 ribs

Leading edge:
- 1"×1" balsa blocks, sanded to rounded profile
- 12 blocks per wing (24 total)

Trailing edge:
- 1/4"×1/4" pine strips
- Continuous strip along trailing edge
```

Step 3: Install ribs on spar notches (every 400mm)

## PHASE 3: TAIL SURFACES

### Step 3.1: Horizontal Stabilizer

```
HORIZONTAL STABILIZER ASSEMBLY:
───────────────────────────────

Step 1: Cut spar (1×2" spruce, 2000mm)
Step 2: Cut 8 ribs (1/4"×1" spruce, Clark Y profile)
Step 3: Assemble spar + ribs + leading/trailing edge
Step 4: Install elevator hinge line at 75% chord
Step 5: Cover with Dacron fabric

DIMENSIONS:
- Span: 2000mm
- Chord: 500mm
- Area: 1.0 m² (10.8 sq ft)

ELEVATOR:
- Span: 1800mm (within HS span)
- Chord: 150mm (30% of HS chord)
- Hinge: 3× AN3-4A clevis bolts
- Deflection: ±25°
```

### Step 3.2: Vertical Stabilizer

```
VERTICAL STABILIZER ASSEMBLY:
─────────────────────────────

Step 1: Cut spar (1×2" spruce, 1200mm)
Step 2: Cut 6 ribs (1/4"×1" spruce, symmetrical profile)
Step 3: Assemble spar + ribs + leading/trailing edge
Step 4: Install rudder hinge line at 75% chord
Step 5: Cover with Dacron fabric

DIMENSIONS:
- Height: 1200mm
- Chord: 800mm
- Area: 0.96 m² (10.3 sq ft)

RUDDER:
- Height: 1100mm (within VS height)
- Chord: 200mm (25% of VS chord)
- Hinge: 3× AN3-4A clevis bolts
- Deflection: ±30°
```
