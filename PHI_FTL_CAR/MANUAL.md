### How Does It Travel to Other Dimensions?

1. **BATTERY** — The FPB-80 battery creates special sound waves
2. **WARP FIELD** — These sound waves bend space around the car
3. **DIMENSION** — The car slips through a portal to another dimension!

## How to Jump to Another Dimension

```
STEP-BY-STEP:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Make sure all doors are closed
  2. All passengers buckled in
  3. Check battery (need at least 20%)
  4. Pick a dimension (D0-D6)
  5. Press the dimension button
  6. Press JUMP
  7. Wait 2 seconds (count: 1... 2!)
  8. You're in the new dimension!
  
  To come home: Press HOME button
  
  Emergency: Press the big RED RETURN button
```
