# PHI OCEAN VEHICLE — Safety Systems

## Safety Philosophy
Ocean operations carry risks from weather, collisions, capsizing, and equipment failure. The Ocean Vehicle's safety system addresses each with redundant, independent protections.

## Multi-Layer Safety Architecture

### Layer 1: Hull Integrity
- Watertight bulkheads (3 compartments)
- Automatic bilge pumps (2x redundant)
- Hull breach detection sensors
- Emergency flotation (foam-filled compartments)
- Self-righting capability (low center of gravity)

### Layer 2: Navigation Safety
- Radar and sonar collision avoidance
- AIS transponder (automatic identification)
- GPS tracking and distress alerting
- Automatic vessel shutdown on imminent collision
- Weather monitoring and storm avoidance

### Layer 3: Passenger Safety
- 6-point safety harnesses (6 locations)
- Life jackets (6 included)
- EPIRB (emergency position-indicating radio beacon)
- flares and signaling equipment
- First aid kit

### Layer 4: Emergency Systems
- Emergency flotation (all compartments)
- 433 MHz distress beacon
- VHF radio distress call
- Manual steering override
- Emergency anchor deployment

## Capsize Safety
- **Self-Righting:** Low center of gravity ensures righting moment
- **Watertight:** All compartments sealed when capsized
- **Air Supply:** Trapped air in cabin provides 30+ minutes
- **Survival Kit:** Water, food, signaling in seat compartments
- **Inversion:** Field propulsion works upside down

## Weather Safety
- **Monitoring:** Real-time weather via satellite
- **Warnings:** Automatic alerts for storms
- **Response:** Auto-navigate to shelter
- **Emergency:** Deploy drogue for stability

## Man Overboard
- **Detection:** Weight sensors detect person leaving
- **Alert:** Alarm sounds, GPS marks position
- **Response:** Auto-circle at safe distance
- **Recovery:** Ladder deploys at stern
- **EPIRB:** Auto-deploy if person in water >5 minutes

## Inspection Protocol
- Pre-departure inspection (10 minutes)
- Automated self-test on power-up (3 minutes)
- Hull integrity check
- Field skimmer function test
- Navigation system verification
- Communication test
- Safety equipment check
