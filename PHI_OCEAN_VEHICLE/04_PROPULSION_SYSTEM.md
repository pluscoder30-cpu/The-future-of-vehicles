# PHI OCEAN VEHICLE — Propulsion System

## Dual-Mode Propulsion
The Ocean Vehicle operates in two distinct modes:

### Mode 1: Surface Cruising
- Phi-harmonic drag reduction active
- Field propulsion drives vehicle through water
- Zero wake, zero noise, zero environmental impact
- Top speed: 80 km/h
- Range: 2,000 km

### Mode 2: Subsurface Operation
- Full drag reduction field (360 degrees)
- Depth control via bow/stern thrusters
- Top speed: 40 km/h
- Maximum depth: 50m

## Maneuvering
- **Forward/Reverse:** Bow and stern thrusters
- **Lateral:** Side thrusters (docking)
- **Depth:** Bow and stern pitch control
- **Rotation:** Asymmetric thruster firing
- **Station Keeping:** All thrusters balanced

## Wake Elimination
The phi-harmonic drag reduction field eliminates wake by:
1. Guiding water molecules along golden-ratio spirals
2. Preventing turbulent separation at hull edges
3. Eliminating pressure drag (form drag)
4. Reducing frictional drag to near-zero
5. No wake means no shoreline erosion, no marine life disturbance

## Environmental Impact

- **Noise:** <30 dB (quieter than natural ocean)
- **Emissions:** Zero (electric)
- **Chemical:** Zero (no antifouling paint needed)
- **Marine Life:** Undisturbed (field invisible to biology)
