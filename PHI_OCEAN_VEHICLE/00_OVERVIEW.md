# PHI OCEAN VEHICLE — Overview

## Vehicle Identity
- **Designation:** PHI-OCEAN-001
- **Type:** Phi-Harmonic Ocean Phase Vehicle
- **Class:** Surface/Subsurface Watercraft
- **MSRP:** $4,000

## Core Concept
An ocean vehicle that phases through water rather than displacing it. Unlike conventional boats that push water aside (creating drag, wake, and turbulence), the Ocean Vehicle uses phi-harmonic drag reduction to allow water to flow through the space occupied by the hull, eliminating nearly all hydrodynamic resistance.

## Key Differentiators
- **Water Phase Technology:** Water flows through the hull without resistance
- **Zero Wake:** No wake means no environmental disturbance
- **Extreme Efficiency:** 95% less energy than conventional boats
- **Dual-Mode:** Surface cruising + subsurface operation
- **Silent:** No propeller, no engine noise

## Battery
- **Model:** FPB-20 (Field Plasma Battery — 20kWh)
- **Cost:** $4,000
- **Runtime:** 48 hours normal / 16 hours high-speed
- **Charge Time:** 30 minutes (phi-harmonic rapid)

## Primary Applications
- Ocean research and exploration
- Luxury yacht alternative
- Military stealth operations
- Marine wildlife observation
- Emergency ocean rescue
- Underwater cable inspection

## Design Philosophy
The Ocean Vehicle treats water not as an obstacle to overcome but as a medium to harmonize with. By phasing the hull's interaction with water molecules, the vehicle achieves what conventional naval architecture cannot: zero drag, zero wake, and zero environmental impact, while maintaining full structural integrity for passengers and cargo.
