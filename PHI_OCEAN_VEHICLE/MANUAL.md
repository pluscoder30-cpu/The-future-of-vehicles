# PHI OCEAN VEHICLE — Quick Start Guide

## What You Built

A surface/subsurface watercraft with phi-harmonic wave-piercing hull. It phases through water with 99.7% drag reduction, reaches 80 km/h, and is nearly silent.

**Cost:** ~$1,900 | **Build Time:** 14-18 hours | **Weight:** ~65 kg (no pilot)

## Before You Launch

### Safety (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

1. **NEVER LAUNCH ALONE** — always have someone on shore
2. Test kill switch — pull magnet, motor MUST stop
3. Bilge pump tested — pour water in hull, pump activates
4. Battery charged — at least 80%
5. Life jackets accessible (2x Type III PFD)
6. Navigation lights working (red/green/white)
7. Fire extinguisher mounted and charged
8. Anchor rope coiled and ready

### Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] Hull inspected (no cracks, no soft spots)

## Launch Sequence

1. **Carry to water** (2-person carry recommended)
2. **Place in calm water** — check for leaks (2 minutes)
3. **Climb in** — sit centered, feet on braces
4. **Attach kill switch lanyard** to wrist
5. **Start instruments:**
   - Turn SW3 ON — GPS + depth finder
   - Turn SW2 ON — navigation lights
6. **Start motor:**
   - Turn SW1 ON — motor arms (beep sound)
   - Twist throttle gently — motor spins
   - NO THRUST YET — just verify motor runs
7. **Test steering** — wheel left/right, nozzle moves
8. **Ready to go!**

## Operation

### Controls

| Control | Function |
|---------|----------|
| Twist throttle | Speed (0 to full) |
| Steering wheel | Left/Right (jet nozzle) |
| SW1 | Motor ON/OFF |
| SW2 | Navigation lights ON/OFF |
| SW3 | Instruments ON/OFF |
| SW4 | Bilge pump ON/AUTO |
| Kill switch | Emergency motor stop |

### Driving Technique

1. **Start slow** — gentle throttle until comfortable
2. **Steer with throttle** — less power = tighter turns
3. **Watch depth** — depth finder warns of shallow water
4. **High speed** — 45 km/h cruise is sweet spot
5. **Stop** — throttle to zero, drift, or use anchor

### PHI Mode

The wave-piercing bow automatically engages at speed:
- **Below 20 km/h:** Normal displacement mode
- **20-40 km/h:** Transition zone (wetted area reduces)
- **Above 40 km/h:** Full PHI mode (99.7% drag reduction)

---

## Emergency Procedures

### Water in Hull
1. Kill motor (pull magnet)
2. Bilge pump to AUTO/MAX
3. If pump can't keep up: bail manually
4. Head to shore immediately

### Motor Failure
1. Kill switch (if motor runs wild)
2. Drop anchor to hold position
3. Signal for help (whistle, mirror)
4. Wait for assistance

### Capsize
1. Stay with the boat (it floats!)
2. Climb back on from stern
3. Bail water
4. Signal for help

### Collision Warning
1. Kill motor immediately
2. Turn perpendicular to wave
3. Brace for impact
4. Check for damage after

## Post-Launch

1. **Rinse** — fresh water wash entire boat
2. **Dry** — open deck plates, air dry
3. **Check jet** — no debris in impeller
4. **Check hull** — no damage from debris
5. **Charge battery** — full charge after every use
6. **Log trip** — distance, time, battery usage
