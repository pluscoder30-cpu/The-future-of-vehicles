# PHI OCEAN VEHICLE — Design Principles

## Water Phase Theory
Conventional boats displace water, creating drag, wake, and turbulence. The Ocean Vehicle uses a phi-harmonic drag reduction field that modifies the hull-water interface at the molecular level. Water molecules follow golden-ratio spiral paths around and through the hull, flowing freely without creating resistive forces.

```
D_eff = D_base × (1 - (Q × sin(φ × ω × t))²)³
```
Where the cubic term creates the near-total elimination:
- At Q=50: 95% drag reduction
- At Q=100: 99.7% drag reduction
- Effective drag approaches zero at optimal Q

## Core Design Tenets

### 1. Environmental Harmony
The vehicle produces no wake, no noise, and no pollution. Marine life is undisturbed by its passage.

### 2. Speed Without Power
With 99% drag reduction, the vehicle achieves high speeds with minimal energy. A 50 km/h cruise requires only 200W — less than a light bulb.

### 3. Depth Independence
The drag reduction field works at any depth, enabling seamless transition from surface cruising to subsurface exploration.

### 4. Structural Integrity
While water flows through the field envelope, the hull itself remains completely solid and watertight. Passengers and cargo are fully protected.
