# PHI EMOTION CLOAK — Structural Design

## Fabric Architecture
The Emotion Cloak uses a multi-layer nano-photonic fabric that combines emotional display with comfortable wearability.

### Layer 1: Comfort Layer (Inner)
- **Material:** Organic cotton-silk blend
- **Function:** Comfortable against skin
- **Features:** Moisture-wicking, breathable

### Layer 2: Sensor Layer
- **Material:** Conductive polymer mesh
- **Function:** Houses biofeedback sensors
- **Features:** Flexible, washable

### Layer 3: Processing Layer
- **Material:** Flexible PCB substrate
- **Function:** Houses AI processor and battery
- **Features:** Bendable, lightweight

### Layer 4: Display Layer (Outer)
- **Material:** Nano-photonic electroluminescent fabric
- **Function:** Color display (16 million colors)
- **Features:** Self-emitting, 60 Hz refresh

### Layer 5: Protection Layer
- **Material:** Graphene-reinforced polymer
- **Function:** Weather and abrasion resistance
- **Features:** Waterproof (IP67), UV-resistant

## Phi-Spiral Draping
The cloak's cut follows phi-spiral geometry:
- **Drape:** Natural golden-ratio folds
- **Movement:** Fabric flows with wearer's motion
- **Pattern:** Color ripples follow drape lines
- **Aesthetic:** Organic, flowing appearance

## Fitting
- **Closure:** Magnetic phi-lock clasp at collar
- **Adjustment:** Drawstring waist for fit
- **Sleeves:** Optional snap-on sleeves
- **Hood:** Optional snap-on hood with face panel
- **Length:** Knee-length (adjustable hem)

## Care Instructions
- Machine wash cold (gentle cycle)
- Mild detergent (no bleach)
- Air dry flat (no heat)
- Do not iron display layer
- Do not dry clean
- Store flat or hanging (no folding on display)
