## 1. Introduction
The PHI Emotion Cloak is a wearable device that changes color based on your emotional state. Using 6 biofeedback sensors and phi-harmonic filtering, it translates your inner feelings into beautiful, harmonious color patterns.

## 2. Safety Information
**NOTE:** This is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

### Before First Use
- Charge battery fully (20 minutes on phi-harmonic dock)
- Wash in cold water (machine wash, gentle cycle)
- Run calibration via app (5 minutes)

### Operating Limitations
- Not suitable for children under 13
- Photosensitive epilepsy: Consult doctor before use
- Do not use as sole medical monitoring device

## 3. Getting Started
1. Unfold cloak from packaging
2. Charge battery: Place on phi-harmonic dock (20 minutes)
3. Download app: Search "Phi Emotion Cloak"
4. Pair via Bluetooth: Open app, follow prompts
5. Run calibration: 5-minute guided process
6. Wear and enjoy

## 4. Wearing the Cloak
1. Drape cloak over shoulders
2. Fasten phi-lock clasp at collar
3. Adjust drawstring waist for comfort
4. Optional: Attach sleeves or hood
5. Ensure sensor contact points touch skin

## 5. Understanding Colors
The cloak displays colors based on your emotional state:
| Color | Emotion | What It Means |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Gold | Joy | You're happy, excited |
| Blue | Calm | You're peaceful, relaxed |
| Indigo | Sadness | You're down, melancholy |
| Red | Anger | You're frustrated, upset |
| White | Fear | You're anxious, worried |
| Pink | Love | You're affectionate, warm |
| Orange | Surprise | You're startled, amazed |
| Green | Disgust | You're repulsed, uncomfortable |
| Gray | Neutral | You're at baseline |

Colors blend and shift — you may see combinations.

## 6. Modes
- **Normal Mode:** Displays your emotions transparently
- **Privacy Mode:** Shows neutral gray (hides emotions)
- **Social Mode:** Shares emotions with nearby cloaks
- **Override Mode:** Fixed color of your choice
- **Sleep Mode:** Minimal display, sensors active

## 7. Social Mode
When multiple Emotion Cloaks are nearby:
1. Both cloaks must opt in
2. Each person's emotions are shared
3. You see a blend of group emotions
4. Opt out anytime with one button press

## 8. App Features
- **Dashboard:** Real-time emotion display
- **History:** Track emotional patterns over time
- **Sensitivity:** Adjust sensor sensitivity
- **Calibration:** Recalibrate sensors
- **Privacy:** Manage who sees your emotions
- **Social:** Control Social Mode settings

## 9. Calibration
First-time calibration takes 5 minutes:
1. Sit comfortably, breathe normally
2. App records baseline (2 minutes)
3. App prompts 5 emotions (happy, sad, angry, calm, surprise)
4. Cloak learns your unique physiology
5. Calibration complete

## 10. Care and Washing
1. Close phi-lock clasp
2. Place in mesh laundry bag (included)
3. Machine wash cold, gentle cycle
4. Mild detergent (no bleach)
5. Air dry flat (24 hours)
6. Do not iron, tumble dry, or dry clean

## 11. Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| No color change | Sensors not contacting skin | Adjust fit |
| Wrong colors | Calibration drift | Recalibrate |
| Flickering | Low battery | Charge battery |
| No BLE connection | Phone out of range | Move closer |
| Red flash | Fault detected | Restart cloak |

## 12. Emergency Features
- **Medical Alert:** Detects abnormal heart rhythms
- **Panic Detection:** Guides breathing exercise
- **Faint Detection:** Alerts emergency contact
- **Quick Release:** Breakaway clasp for safety
