# PHI EMOTION CLOAK — Safety Systems

## Safety Philosophy
The Emotion Cloak is a wearable device that reads biological signals. Safety focuses on privacy, data security, and ensuring the device never causes emotional harm.

## Safety Architecture

### Physical Safety
- No harmful EMF (passive sensors only)
- No skin irritation (hypoallergenic materials)
- No overheating (maximum 32C surface temperature)
- No entanglement (breakaway clasp design)
- No obstructed vision (hood optional)

### Emotional Safety
- **Privacy Mode:** Always available (one button press)
- **Override Mode:** Fixed color to hide emotions
- **Sensitivity Control:** Reduce emotional display intensity
- **Calibration:** Accurate emotion mapping prevents misrepresentation
- **Phi-Harmonic Filter:** Prevents chaotic emotional display

### Data Security
- All processing on-device (no cloud dependency)
- No emotional data transmitted without consent
- Social sharing requires explicit opt-in
- History encrypted with device key
- Factory reset erases all data

### Medical Safety
- **Not a medical device:** Does not diagnose or treat conditions
- **Heart rate monitoring:** For emotional awareness only
- **Emergency Alert:** Detects abnormal heart rhythms (advises medical consultation)
- **Epilepsy Warning:** Rapid color changes may affect photosensitive individuals (reduce sensitivity)

## Social Safety
- **Consent Required:** Social Mode requires both parties to opt in
- **No Surveillance:** Cloak cannot record or transmit audio/video
- **Anonymous Mode:** Share emotions without identity
- **Blocking:** Block specific cloaks from receiving your emotions
- **Time Limits:** Auto-disable Social Mode after 8 hours

## Inspection Protocol
- Visual inspection before each wear
- Sensor function check via app
- Battery level verification
- Fabric integrity check (no tears or damage)
- Firmware update check
