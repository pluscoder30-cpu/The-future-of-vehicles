# PHI EMOTION CLOAK — Maintenance Guide

## Maintenance Philosophy
The Emotion Cloak is a wearable device designed for daily use. The fabric is washable, and the solid-state electronics require minimal maintenance.

## Maintenance Schedule

### Before Each Wear
- [ ] Visual inspection (no tears or damage)
- [ ] Battery level check (via app)
- [ ] Sensor function check (via app)
- [ ] Clean contact points (if needed)

### Weekly
- [ ] Wipe sensors with damp cloth
- [ ] Check phi-lock clasp function
- [ ] Verify BLE connection to phone
- [ ] Update firmware (if available)

### Monthly
- [ ] Full sensor calibration (via app)
- [ ] Battery full cycle test
- [ ] Fabric integrity inspection
- [ ] Check waterproofing (no damage)

### As Needed
- [ ] Machine wash (cold, gentle)
- [ ] Air dry flat (no heat)
- [ ] Replace drawstring (if worn)
- [ ] Repair minor fabric damage

## Washing Instructions
1. Close phi-lock clasp
2. Place in mesh laundry bag (included)
3. Machine wash cold, gentle cycle
4. Mild detergent (no bleach, no fabric softener)
5. Air dry flat (no tumble dryer)
6. Do not iron
7. Do not dry clean
8. Allow 24 hours to fully dry

## Sensor Care
- Wipe sensors with damp cloth after heavy use
- Do not submerge sensor areas in water
- If sensors malfunction, run calibration via app
- Contact service if calibration fails

## Battery Care
- Charge before first use
- Store at 50% charge for long-term
- Do not expose to extreme heat (>50C)
- Battery lasts 2+ years with normal use

## Diagnostic Mode
Hold phi-lock clasp for 10 seconds:
- **Green flash:** All systems nominal
- **Yellow flash:** Maintenance due
- **Red flash:** Fault detected
- **Color cycle:** Sensors active
