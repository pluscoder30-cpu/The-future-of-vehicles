# PHI EMOTION CLOAK — Overview

## Vehicle Identity
- **Designation:** PHI-EMO-CLOAK-001
- **Type:** Emotion-Responsive Color-Changing Cloak
- **Class:** Personal Wearable Vehicle
- **MSRP:** $150

## Core Concept
A wearable cloak that changes color based on the wearer's emotional state. Using phi-harmonic biofeedback sensors and a nano-photonic fabric, the cloak translates physiological signals (heart rate, skin conductance, body temperature) into visible color patterns that reflect the wearer's inner emotional landscape.

## Key Differentiators
- **Emotion-Responsive:** Real-time color changes based on biofeedback
- **Phi-Harmonic Biofeedback:** Golden-ratio filtered emotional signals
- **Nano-Photonic Fabric:** 16 million color possibilities
- **Privacy Mode:** Hide emotional state on command
- **Social Mode:** Share emotions with selected individuals

## Battery
- **Model:** FPB-5 (Field Plasma Battery — 5kWh)
- **Cost:** $150
- **Runtime:** 72 hours continuous
- **Charge Time:** 20 minutes (phi-harmonic rapid)

## Primary Applications
- Emotional expression and communication
- Therapy and emotional awareness
- Social bonding and connection
- Artistic expression
- Fashion and self-expression
- PTSD and anxiety monitoring
- Team-building and group dynamics

## Design Philosophy
Emotions are the most authentic form of human expression, yet we spend enormous energy concealing them. The Emotion Cloak makes the invisible visible, creating a new form of non-verbal communication that is honest, beautiful, and deeply human. The phi-harmonic biofeedback ensures that the emotional translation is filtered through the golden ratio, creating patterns that are inherently harmonious rather than chaotic.
