# PHI EMOTION CLOAK — Design Principles

## Emotional Biofeedback Theory
Human emotions produce measurable physiological signatures: heart rate variability, skin conductance, body temperature, muscle tension, and respiration rate. The Emotion Cloak captures these signals and translates them into color patterns using phi-harmonic filtering.

## The Emotion-Color Mapping Equation
```
Color = φ-filter(Σ(w_i × Signal_i))
```
Where:
- Signal_i = physiological signal (HR, GSR, Temp, etc.)
- w_i = weight for each signal
- φ-filter = golden-ratio smoothing filter
- Color = HSL value mapped to emotional state

## Core Design Tenets

### 1. Authentic Expression
The cloak cannot lie. It translates genuine physiological signals, not conscious choices. This creates a form of communication that is honest and unguarded.

### 2. Harmonious Translation
The phi-harmonic filter ensures that even intense emotions are translated into visually harmonious patterns. Rage becomes a deep, pulsing crimson with golden undertones — intense but beautiful.

### 3. Privacy Control
The wearer can activate Privacy Mode at any time, which displays a neutral gray or a chosen "public face" color. Emotional transparency is always voluntary.

### 4. Social Connection
In Social Mode, the cloak broadcasts emotional state to nearby Emotion Cloaks, creating a shared emotional landscape. Groups can literally see how they feel together.

## Aesthetic Language
- **Form:** Flowing cloak with phi-spiral draping
- **Default Color:** Deep midnight blue with subtle phi-trace shimmer
- **Texture:** Soft, silk-like nano-photonic fabric
- **Illumination:** Self-emitting (no external light needed)
- **Movement:** Color ripples follow the wearer's motion
