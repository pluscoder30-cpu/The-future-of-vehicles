## LOADING PROCEDURE

```
FREIGHT LOADING CHECKLIST:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. INSPECT TRAILER
     □ Floor clear of debris
     □ Tie-down points accessible (30×)
     □ Cargo lights functional
     □ Roll-up doors operational
     □ Loading ramp functional

  2. LOAD FREIGHT (use crane for >500 kg)
     □ Heaviest items first (floor level)
     □ Distribute weight 35/65 (front/rear)
     □ Leave access to emergency exits
     □ Secure with phi-locks (minimum 6)

  3. VERIFY LOAD
     □ Total weight < 50,000 kg
     □ Weight distribution verified
     □ No items blocking doors
     □ All tie-downs engaged
     □ Phi-locks activated

  4. PRE-JUMP CHECK
     □ Cargo shift sensor: GREEN
     □ Load balance: GREEN
     □ Door locks: ENGAGED
     □ Emergency release: ACCESSIBLE
     □ Trailer coupling: VERIFIED
```
