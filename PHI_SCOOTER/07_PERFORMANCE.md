## Limitations

1. **Small wheels** — less stable on rough terrain
2. **Limited by weight** — heavier riders reduce range significantly
3. **Weather dependent** — rain reduces traction
4. **No hill climbing** — steep hills drain battery quickly
5. **Charging time** — 3 hours for full charge
6. **Not foldable with motor** — motor adds weight to folding mechanism
