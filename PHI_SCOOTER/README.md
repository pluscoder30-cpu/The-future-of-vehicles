## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$400
- **Speed**: 25 km/h (15.5 mph)
- **Range**: 25 km (15.5 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 100 kg (220 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 3 hours
- **Motor**: 350W phi-harmonic brushless front hub motor

## What Makes It Special

A lightweight electric scooter with a 350W phi-harmonic front hub motor. The golden-angle magnet arrangement reduces cogging by 65%, making the scooter virtually silent and extending range by 32% compared to conventional scooters.
