# PHI_SCOOTER — Overview

## What It Is

A lightweight electric scooter with a 350W phi-harmonic front hub motor, designed for urban commuting and recreational riding. The motor uses golden-angle (137.5°) magnet spacing for silent operation and maximum efficiency.

## What It Does

- Electric propulsion up to 25 km/h (15.5 mph)
- Thumb throttle for motor-only operation
- 3 speed modes (Eco, Normal, Sport)
- LED display shows speed, battery, mode
- Front and rear mechanical disc brakes
- Foldable stem for easy carrying

## How It Works (Simplified)

1. **Throttle**: Thumb throttle sends signal to controller
2. **Controller**: Calculates motor power based on throttle position + speed mode
3. **Phi-Harmonic Motor**: Golden-angle magnets produce smooth, efficient rotation
4. **Battery**: 36V Samsung cells provide 280 Wh capacity
5. **Regenerative Braking**: Front motor acts as generator during braking, recovering energy
