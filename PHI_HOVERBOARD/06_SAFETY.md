# PHI_HOVERBOARD — Safety Precautions

## CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS

> ⚠️ **READ ALL WARNINGS BEFORE OPERATING THE HOVERBOARD**
> ⚠️ **FAILURE TO FOLLOW SAFETY RULES CAN RESULT IN SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH**

---

## Electrical Safety

### Battery Hazards
- **LiFePO4 batteries store significant energy** — a short circuit can cause fire
- Never expose battery to temperatures above 60°C (140°F)
- Never puncture, crush, or disassemble battery cells
- If battery swells, smells, or smokes — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY** and move to open air
- Keep away from flammable materials during charging
- **Charge only with the supplied 48V LiFePO4 charger** — never use other chargers
- Charge in a well-ventilated area, not on beds or couches
- Unplug charger when fully charged

### High Voltage
- The battery operates at **48V DC** — this can cause burns or cardiac arrest
- Never touch battery terminals with bare hands
- Always disconnect battery before working on electronics
- Use insulated tools when working near live circuits
- Wear rubber-soled shoes when operating the board
- If you feel a tingle or shock — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY**

### MOSFET Heat
- MOSFETs can reach temperatures up to 80°C during operation
- Never touch MOSFETs while the board is powered
- Allow 30 minutes cool-down before handling internal components
- If you smell burning plastic — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY**

---

## Mechanical Safety

### Levitation Risks
- The board levitates 8-12mm — it is NOT stable at rest
- **Always step on with one foot first**, then the other
- Keep feet centered on the foot pads
- Never jump or hop on the board
- Never lean backward — the board will shoot forward
- Keep weight over the center of the board at all times
- The board will fall if power is lost — be prepared to catch yourself

### Surface Requirements
- **ONLY use on steel surfaces** (minimum 3mm thick)
- Never use on concrete, wood, tile, or asphalt without steel underlayer
- Check surface for debris, water, or oil before each use
- Keep 1 meter away from edges of steel surfaces
- Never use on surfaces with gaps, joints, or uneven sections

### Speed & Distance
- Maximum speed: 20 km/h (12.4 mph) — this is fast enough to cause injury
- Always wear protective gear (helmet, knee pads, elbow pads)
- Keep speed below 10 km/h until you are experienced
- Never ride in traffic, near pedestrians, or in enclosed spaces
- Maintain 3 meters of clearance from all obstacles

## Emergency Procedures

### Board Malfunction
1. **Immediately step off the board** (both feet at once)
2. Move away from the board
3. If board is still powered, press the emergency stop button
4. If board is smoking or sparking, move at least 5 meters away
5. Call for help if anyone is injured

### Battery Emergency
1. If battery smokes or catches fire: **DO NOT USE WATER**
2. Move away from the area
3. Use a Class D fire extinguisher (metal fire) if available
4. Call emergency services (911)
5. If skin contact with battery acid, flush with water for 15 minutes

### Fall Response
1. If you fall, try to roll with the fall (don't extend arms)
2. Assess for injuries before standing
3. If you suspect a fracture, do not move the injured area
4. Call for help if needed

## Maintenance Safety

### Before Each Ride
- [ ] Check battery voltage (should be >45V)
- [ ] Check all bolts are tight
- [ ] Check coils for damage or loose wires
- [ ] Check foot pads are secure
- [ ] Check emergency stop button works
- [ ] Test on steel surface briefly before full use

### Weekly Maintenance
- [ ] Clean coils with dry cloth
- [ ] Check all wiring connections
- [ ] Test battery balance with multimeter
- [ ] Check MOSFET temperatures after 5-minute run
- [ ] Update firmware if available

### Monthly Maintenance
- [ ] Full battery charge cycle (discharge to 20%, charge to 100%)
- [ ] Check all solder joints for cracks
- [ ] Inspect frame for cracks or damage
- [ ] Test all sensors with multimeter
- [ ] Clean and re-grip tape if worn
