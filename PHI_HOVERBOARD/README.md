## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$800
- **Speed**: 20 km/h (12.4 mph)
- **Range**: 15 km (9.3 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 100 kg (220 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 2.5 hours
- **Levitation Height**: 8-12 mm above surface

## What Makes It Special

Uses phi-harmonic (φ = 1.618) electromagnetic coil geometry to generate levitation force through constructive interference patterns. The coils are arranged at golden-angle spacing (137.5°) which maximizes flux density while minimizing mutual inductance losses.
