## NAVIGATION PROCEDURES

### Pre-Jump Checklist
```
1. VERIFY CARGO SECURED
   □ All tie-downs tight
   □ Hazardous materials flagged
   □ Weight distribution verified

2. VERIFY SYSTEMS
   □ FPB-80 SoC > 20%
   □ Warp coils nominal
   □ Dimensional tuner calibrated
   □ Reality anchor active

3. VERIFY ENVIRONMENT
   □ 15m clear radius
   □ No living beings in warp zone
   □ Local interference checked
   □ Weather acceptable

4. SELECT TARGET
   □ Choose dimension (D0-D6)
   □ Set frequency on tuner
   □ Confirm on HUD display
   □ Lock target frequency
```

### Jump Sequence
```
STEP 1: INITIATE (t = 0.0s)
  - Press JUMP button
  - Warp coils energize
  - Field builds to full strength

STEP 2: RESONATE (t = 0.0-1.5s)
  - Coils reach target frequency
  - Resonance stabilizer locks
  - Dimensional HUD shows lock

STEP 3: TRANSITION (t = 1.5-2.3s)
  - Spacetime curvature reaches threshold
  - Vehicle enters warp bubble
  - Reality anchor maintains connection

STEP 4: TRANSIT (variable)
  - Travel at target speed
  - Monitor field stability
  - Adjust course as needed

STEP 5: ARRIVAL (t = 0.0s relative)
  - Reduce field strength
  - Discharge warp bubble
  - Verify dimension
  - Resume normal operations
```
