### How Does It Travel to Other Dimensions?

1. **BATTERY** — The FPB-80 battery creates special sound waves
2. **WARP FIELD** — These sound waves bend space around the truck
3. **DIMENSION** — The truck slips through a portal to another dimension!

## How to Jump to Another Dimension

```
STEP-BY-STEP:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Check your battery (need at least 20%)
  2. Make sure cargo is secure
  3. Pick which dimension you want (D0-D6)
  4. Press the dimension button on the dashboard
  5. Press the JUMP button
  6. Wait 2.3 seconds (count: 1... 2... 3!)
  7. You're in the new dimension!
  
  To come home: Press the HOME button
  
  Emergency: Press the big RED RETURN button
```
