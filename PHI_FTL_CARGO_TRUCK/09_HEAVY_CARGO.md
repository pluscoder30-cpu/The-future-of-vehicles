## LOADING PROCEDURE

```
HEAVY CARGO LOADING CHECKLIST:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. INSPECT CARGO AREA
     □ Floor clear of debris
     □ Tie-down points accessible (20×)
     □ Cargo lights functional
     □ Hydraulic gate operational

  2. LOAD CARGO (use crane for >500 kg)
     □ Heaviest items first (floor level)
     □ Distribute weight 40/60 (front/rear)
     □ Leave access to emergency exits
     □ Secure with phi-locks (minimum 4)

  3. VERIFY LOAD
     □ Total weight < 10,000 kg
     □ Weight distribution verified
     □ No items blocking gates
     □ All tie-downs engaged
     □ Phi-locks activated

  4. PRE-JUMP CHECK
     □ Cargo shift sensor: GREEN
     □ Load balance: GREEN
     □ Gate locks: ENGAGED
     □ Emergency release: ACCESSIBLE
```
