> **READ ALL WARNINGS BEFORE OPERATING THE E-BIKE**
> **FAILURE TO FOLLOW SAFETY RULES CAN RESULT IN SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH**

---

## Electrical Safety

### Battery Hazards
- **LiFePO4 batteries store significant energy** — a short circuit can cause fire
- Never expose battery to temperatures above 60°C (140°F)
- Never puncture, crush, or disassemble battery cells
- If battery swells, smells, or smokes — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY** and move to open air
- Keep away from flammable materials during charging
- **Charge only with the supplied 36V LiFePO4 charger** — never use other chargers
- Charge in a well-ventilated area, not on beds or couches
- Unplug charger when fully charged

### High Voltage
- The battery operates at **36V DC** — this can cause burns
- Never touch battery terminals with bare hands
- Always disconnect battery before working on electronics
- Use insulated tools when working near live circuits

### Motor Heat
- Hub motor can reach temperatures up to 65°C during extended hill climbing
- Never touch motor immediately after riding
- Allow 15 minutes cool-down before handling

## Riding Safety

### Traffic Laws
- **Obey all traffic laws** — stop signs, traffic lights, yield to pedestrians
- Ride in the same direction as traffic
- Use hand signals for turns
- Stay in bike lanes where available
- Maximum speed: 25 km/h (15.5 mph) — do not exceed local limits
- **Never ride on highways or freeways**

### Braking
- Both brakes should be tested before each ride
- Front brake provides 70% of stopping power
- Rear brake provides 30% of stopping power
- Use both brakes together for maximum stopping
- **Never brake suddenly** — progressive braking prevents skids
- Reduce speed before turns

### Pedaling
- Pedal assist levels: 0 (off), 1-5 (increasing power)
- Start at level 1 until familiar with motor response
- Level 5 provides full power — be cautious on first use
- Cadence sensor requires continuous pedaling for assist
- Thumb throttle provides full power without pedaling — use carefully

### Weight & Load
- Maximum rider weight: 100 kg (220 lbs)
- Do not carry heavy backpacks while riding
- Do not carry passengers — this is a single-rider bike

## Emergency Procedures

### Bike Malfunction
1. **Immediately release throttle**
2. Apply brakes gently and come to a stop
3. Step off the bike
4. Check for issues before continuing

### Battery Emergency
1. If battery smokes or catches fire: **DO NOT USE WATER**
2. Move away from the area
3. Use a Class D fire extinguisher (metal fire) if available
4. Call emergency services (911)

### Fall Response
1. If you fall, try to roll with the fall (don't extend arms)
2. Assess for injuries before standing
3. If you suspect a fracture, do not move the injured area
4. Call for help if needed

## Maintenance Safety

### Before Each Ride
- [ ] Check battery charge (should be >30V)
- [ ] Check tire pressure (40 PSI)
- [ ] Check brakes function properly
- [ ] Check all quick-releases are locked
- [ ] Check handlebars are tight
- [ ] Check lights work

### Weekly Maintenance
- [ ] Check tire tread wear
- [ ] Check chain for rust or stiffness
- [ ] Check brake pad wear
- [ ] Check all bolts are tight
- [ ] Clean frame and components

### Monthly Maintenance
- [ ] Full battery charge cycle (discharge to 20%, charge to 100%)
- [ ] Check spoke tension
- [ ] Check derailleur adjustment
- [ ] Lubricate chain
- [ ] Check fold joint for wear

## Folding Safety

- **Always lock all quick-releases** before riding
- Check fold joint is secure before each ride
- Do not ride if fold joint feels loose
- Lubricate fold joint quarterly
- Replace worn fold joint parts immediately
