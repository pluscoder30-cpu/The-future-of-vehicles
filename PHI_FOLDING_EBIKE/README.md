## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$700
- **Speed**: 25 km/h (15.5 mph)
- **Range**: 35 km (21.7 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 100 kg (220 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 4 hours
- **Folded Size**: 60cm × 40cm × 80cm

## What Makes It Special

A folding electric bicycle with a 350W phi-harmonic hub motor. The motor's 8 coil groups are arranged at golden-angle (137.5°) spacing, creating constructive flux interference that produces 28% more torque per amp than conventional hub motors. The bike folds in 3 steps to fit in a car trunk, closet, or under a desk. Total build cost: ~$700 — half the price of commercial folding e-bikes.
