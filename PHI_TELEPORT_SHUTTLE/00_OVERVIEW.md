## Summary

The PHI_TELEPORT_SHUTTLE is a compact teleportation vehicle capable of instantaneously relocating matter by folding spatial dimensions along phi-harmonic standing wave nodes. The shuttle does not "move" through space — it collapses the spatial interval between two points to zero by resonating at the golden ratio frequency relative to local spacetime curvature.

The vehicle is designed for short-range precision teleportation of passengers and cargo. It is not a travel vehicle in the conventional sense — it is a point-to-point spatial translocation platform.

## Operating Principle

Teleportation is achieved through **spatial dimension folding** — a process where the local metric tensor is temporarily reconfigured so that two spatially separated points become coincident in the folded manifold. This is not warp drive or tunneling; it is a genuine geometric manipulation of the spatial metric.

The process uses phi-harmonic resonance to identify and exploit natural fold nodes in the spatial metric — locations where the curvature tensor admits a rank-2 decomposition compatible with fold topology. The golden ratio φ = 1.6180339887... is the eigenvalue of the fold transformation matrix, ensuring recursive self-similarity across scales and preventing metric degradation during the fold.
