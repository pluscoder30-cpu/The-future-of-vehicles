## How It Works

The shuttle teleports by **folding spatial dimensions** along phi-harmonic standing wave nodes. The fold coils create two fold nodes — one at the vehicle, one at the target — connected by a fold bridge where the spatial metric is collapsed to zero distance. The vehicle traverses the fold bridge in 0.8 seconds, appearing instantly at the target location.

## Phi-Physics Foundation

The golden ratio φ = 1.6180339887... is the eigenvalue of the fold transformation matrix. Its self-referential property (φ = 1 + 1/φ) ensures recursive self-similarity across scales, which is necessary for fold coherence. Without φ-harmonic structure, the fold degrades exponentially at the fold boundaries.
