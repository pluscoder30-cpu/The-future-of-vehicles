## WHAT IS THE PHI AI WATER DRONE?

The PHI AI Water Drone is a flying robot with AI that cleans water! It uses sensors to find pollution, AI to figure out the best cleaning path, and filters to clean the water. It can work with other drones to clean big lakes and rivers!

### How Does It Clean Water?

The drone has FOUR ways to help:

1. **FINDS POLLUTION** — Sensors detect chemicals and particles
2. **MAPS CONTAMINATION** — AI creates pollution heatmap
3. **CLEANS WATER** — 3-stage filtration system
4. **OPTIMIZES PATH** — AI finds fastest cleaning route

## How to Use the AI Features

### Starting a Water Cleaning Mission

```
AI WATER CLEANING:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Charge battery fully
  2. Clean/replace filters
  3. Turn on drone
  4. Wait for AI to boot
  5. Connect phone to WiFi
  6. Open drone app
  7. Press "AI SURVEY"
  8. AI maps contamination
  9. Review AI cleaning plan
  10. Press "START CLEANING"
  11. AI guides cleaning path
  12. Monitor quality improvement
```
