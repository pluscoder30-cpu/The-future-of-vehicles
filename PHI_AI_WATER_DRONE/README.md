# PHI AI WATER DRONE

## AI-Powered Water Cleaning Drone

**$328 - FPB-5 Battery - AI Contamination Mapping - Path Optimization - 100% DIY**

---

## WHAT IS THIS?

The PHI AI Water Drone is a dirt-cheap water cleaning drone with AI capabilities you can build for under $330. It maps contamination, optimizes cleaning paths, and coordinates with other water drones.

## AI FEATURES

### Contamination Mapping
- Real-time contamination heatmap
- Pollutant type classification
- Hotspot identification

### Path Optimization
- AI calculates most efficient cleaning route
- Prioritizes high-contamination areas
- Adapts in real-time

### Multi-Drone Coordination
- AI assigns cleaning sectors
- Shares contamination data
- Prevents redundant cleaning
