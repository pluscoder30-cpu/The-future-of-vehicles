## AI CAPABILITIES

### 1. Contamination Mapping
- AI creates real-time contamination map
- Identifies pollution hotspots
- Classifies pollutant type (chemical, organic, sediment)
- Tracks contamination spread

### 2. Cleaning Path Optimization
- AI calculates most efficient cleaning route
- Prioritizes high-contamination areas
- Minimizes overlap and missed spots
- Adapts path based on real-time readings

### 3. Multi-Drone Coordination
- AI assigns cleaning sectors
- Shares contamination data between drones
- Prevents redundant cleaning
- Coordinates filter replacement
