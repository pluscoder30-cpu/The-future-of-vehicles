## AI CLEANING PROCEDURE

| Step | Action | AI Role |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Survey water body | AI generates contamination map |
| 2 | Analyze water quality | AI classifies pollutant type |
| 3 | Plan cleaning path | AI optimizes route for efficiency |
| 4 | Position over water | AI guides drone position |
| 5 | Activate filtration | AI controls pump speed |
| 6 | Apply cleaning frequencies | AI selects optimal frequencies |
| 7 | Monitor improvement | AI tracks quality changes |
| 8 | Adjust path if needed | AI re-optimizes in real-time |
