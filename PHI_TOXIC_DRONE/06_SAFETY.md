## CHEMICAL DETECTION THRESHOLDS

| Chemical | Warning (ppm) | Danger (ppm) | Action |
|----------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| VOC | > 100 | > 500 | Alert, investigate |
| CO | > 25 | > 50 | Alert, evacuate |
| Smoke | Any | > 100 | Alert, investigate |
| pH | < 5 or > 9 | < 4 or > 10 | Alert, corrosive |

---

## NEUTRALIZER USE

| Spill Type | Neutralizer | Application |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| Acid | Baking soda solution | Spray directly |
| Base | Vinegar solution | Spray directly |
| Organic | Activated carbon | Apply slurry |
| Oil | Absorbent material | Drone cannot handle |
| Unknown | DO NOT approach | Call professionals |

---

## POST-OPERATION

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Land drone in isolated area |
| 2 | Wear gloves and mask |
| 3 | Remove neutralizer tank |
| 4 | Clean drone with soap and water |
| 5 | Dispose of collected materials properly |
| 6 | Calibrate sensors before next use |
