# PHI TOXIC DRONE — Kid-Friendly Instruction Manual

## What Is the PHI Toxic Drone?

The PHI Toxic Drone is a flying robot that finds and cleans up dangerous chemicals! It has special sensors that can smell bad chemicals, and it can spray chemicals that make the bad stuff safe. It costs less than $450 to build!

### How Does It Clean Up Chemicals?

1. **SMELLS** — Special sensors detect bad chemicals in the air
2. **ALERTS** — Tells adults about the danger
3. **CLEANS** — Sprays safe chemicals that make the bad stuff harmless
