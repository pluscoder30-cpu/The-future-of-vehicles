# PHI FOOD DRONE

## Portable Phi-Harmonic Food Growing Drone

## WHAT IS THIS?

The PHI Food Drone is a dirt-cheap food-growing drone you can build for under $350. It plants seeds, delivers nutrients, and applies phi-harmonic growth frequencies to grow herbs and vegetables in small spaces like balconies, rooftops, and garden plots.

## PHI-HARMONIC DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Every ratio approaches the Golden Ratio (phi = 1.618033988749894):

- Body Length / Width = 450mm / 278mm = 1.618 = phi
- Herb Bay / Veg Bay = 130mm / 80mm = 1.625 ≈ phi
- Veg Bay / Flower Bay = 80mm / 50mm = 1.600 ≈ phi
- Growth Frequencies: 417Hz (528·Φ⁻²), 528Hz, 639Hz (528·Φ) — phi-ladder
