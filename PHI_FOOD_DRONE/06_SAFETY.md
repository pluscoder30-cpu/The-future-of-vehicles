## FOOD SAFETY

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| Edible nutrients only | Use organic, food-safe liquid nutrients |
| No pesticides | Never apply pesticides with this drone |
| Clean water | Use potable water for nutrient mixing |
| pH monitoring | Keep soil pH 6.0-7.0 for most edibles |
| Harvest timing | Wait for proper maturity before eating |
| Wash produce | Always wash before consumption |

---

## WEATHER CONDITIONS

| Condition | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Clear, calm | Safe |
| Light wind (<15 km/h) | Caution |
| Moderate wind (15-25 km/h) | Risky |
| Strong wind (>25 km/h) | DO NOT FLY |
| Rain | DO NOT FLY |

---

## EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

| Emergency | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Motor failure | Auto-land immediately |
| Nutrient leak | Shut off pump, land, clean |
| Battery low | Return to base |
| Signal loss | Auto-land after 30 sec |
| Propeller damage | Stop motors, inspect |
