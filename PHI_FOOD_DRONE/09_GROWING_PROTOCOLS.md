# PHI FOOD DRONE — GROWING PROTOCOLS

## Food Growing Guidelines

---

## RECOMMENDED FOODS

| Category | Examples | Seed Size | Spacing |
|----------|----------|-----------|---------|
| Leafy Greens | Lettuce, spinach, kale | Small | 10cm |
| Herbs | Basil, cilantro, parsley | Small | 15cm |
| Root Vegetables | Radishes, carrots | Small | 10cm |
| Legumes | Beans, peas | Medium | 20cm |
| Fruiting | Tomatoes, peppers | Medium | 30cm |

---

## GROWING CALENDAR

| Season | What to Plant | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Spring | Lettuce, peas, herbs | After last frost |
| Summer | Tomatoes, beans, peppers | Water frequently |
| Fall | Kale, spinach, radishes | 6 weeks before frost |
| Winter | Indoor only | Not recommended |

---

## SOIL PREPARATION

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Test soil pH (target 6.0-7.0) |
| 2 | Clear debris and weeds |
| 3 | Loosen top 10cm of soil |
| 4 | Add compost if needed |
| 5 | Level surface |

---

## SEED LOADING

```
SEED LOADING PROCEDURE:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Verify seed viability (>80% germination)
  2. Open appropriate hopper
  3. Pour seeds (do not exceed capacity)
  4. Close lid securely
  5. Test dispenser (dry run)
  6. Record seeds loaded in log

  Herb bay: 130g max
  Veg bay: 80g max
  Flower bay: 50g max
```

---

## NUTRIENT MIXING

| Nutrient | Ratio | Purpose |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Nitrogen (N) | 10-10-10 | General growth |
| Phosphorus (P) | 5-10-5 | Root development |
| Potassium (K) | 10-5-10 | Fruit production |

**Mix: 5ml concentrate per 300ml water**

---

## PLANTING MISSION

```
AUTO-PLANTING SEQUENCE:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Set planting coordinates via app
  2. Load seeds and nutrients
  3. Takeoff to 10m
  4. Navigate to area
  5. Descend to 1.5m AGL
  6. Follow grid pattern:
     - Drop seeds (2 sec)
     - Spray nutrients (3 sec)
     - Apply frequency (5 sec)
     - Move to next point
  7. Return to base
  8. Land
```
