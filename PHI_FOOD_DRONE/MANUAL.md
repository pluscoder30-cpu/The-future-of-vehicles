# PHI FOOD DRONE — Kid-Friendly Instruction Manual

## What Is the PHI Food Drone?

The PHI Food Drone is a small flying robot that helps you grow food! It can plant seeds for herbs, vegetables, and flowers, spray plant food, and play special sounds that help plants grow faster. It costs less than $350 to build!

### How Does It Help You Grow Food?

1. **PLANTS SEEDS** — Drops seeds exactly where they need to go
2. **FEEDS PLANTS** — Sprays plant food on each plant
3. **PLAYS GROWTH SOUNDS** — Special sounds help plants grow faster

---

## Quick Start

1. Fill seed hoppers (herbs, veggies, or flowers)
2. Fill nutrient tank with plant food
3. Type your garden location in the app
4. Press "PLANT" and watch the drone work!
5. Press "GROW" to play growth sounds
