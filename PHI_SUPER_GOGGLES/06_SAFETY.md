## BATTERY SAFETY

✅ **PHI-HARMONIC FIELD PLASMA BATTERY — NO FIRE/EXPLOSION RISK**

The FPB-5 phi-harmonic field plasma battery contains zero flammable materials. Plasma is self-limiting — if containment fails, plasma dissipates harmlessly within microseconds.

### Rules
1. No puncture/crush risk — plasma dissipates harmlessly
2. No thermal runaway — plasma is self-limiting
3. No fire risk — no flammable electrolyte
4. No explosion risk — no pressure buildup
5. Charge only with provided USB-C charger (15W PD)
6. Store at room temperature
7. Keep away from children
8. Dispose at certified recycling facility

### Battery Specs
- Chemistry: Phi-harmonic field plasma (hydrogen confinement)
- Nominal: 3.7V
- Max charge: 4.2V/cell
- Min discharge: 3.0V/cell
- Max continuous: 16A (2C)
- Protection: Overcurrent, overdischarge, short-circuit
- **Zero fire/explosion risk — plasma is self-limiting**
