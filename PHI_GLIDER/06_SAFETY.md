## Training Requirements

### Mandatory Training
1. **Ground school**: Minimum 10 hours of hang gliding theory
2. **Training flights**: Minimum 10 flights with certified instructor
3. **Emergency procedures**: Practice motor failure, landing, rescue
4. **Medical clearance**: FAA Class 3 medical or equivalent
5. **Insurance**: Required in most jurisdictions

### Recommended Training
- Solo flight training (20+ hours)
- Thermal flying techniques
- Cross-country navigation
- Night flying (if applicable)

## Emergency Procedures

### Motor Failure in Flight
1. **Stay calm** — you have a glider, not a brick
2. Check altitude — if >50m, you have time
3. Look for landing zone (flat, clear area)
4. Configure for best glide speed (30 km/h)
5. Land into wind
6. Use emergency landing technique (flare at 2m altitude)

### Low Battery Warning
1. **Immediately reduce power** to minimum
2. Look for nearest landing zone
3. Plan straight-in approach
4. Land before battery dies completely
5. Motor will cut off at 30V — be ready

### Structural Failure
1. **Deploy emergency parachute** (if available)
2. Release control bar
3. Assume landing position
4. Try to land in soft area (bushes, water)
5. **Protect your head**

### Wind Shear/Turbulence
1. Reduce power to minimum
2. Maintain control bar pressure
3. Look for smooth air (lee side of hill)
4. Plan emergency landing if needed
5. **Do not fight the wind** — work with it

## Weight & Balance

### Maximum Gross Weight
- Pilot: 90 kg maximum
- Equipment: 15 kg maximum
- **Total: 105 kg maximum**

### Center of Gravity
- Must be within 5cm of design CG
- Adjust battery position to achieve
- Test CG before first flight (hang test)

## Weather Limitations

### Maximum Wind
- Takeoff: 15 km/h (8 knots)
- Flight: 25 km/h (13 knots)
- Landing: 20 km/h (11 knots)

### Minimum Visibility
- 3 km (2 miles) minimum
- No flying in fog, rain, or snow

### Temperature Range
- 0°C to 35°C (32°F to 95°F)
- Cold: Battery performance reduced 20%
- Hot: Motor overheating risk

### Cloud Clearance
- 500m (1640 ft) from clouds
- Do not enter cloud layer

## Legal Requirements

### United States
- Ultralight category (FAR Part 103)
- Maximum speed: 55 km/h (34 mph) — **exceeds limit**
- Registration required as Experimental Amateur-Built
- Pilot certificate required (Sport Pilot minimum)
- Medical certificate required (Sport Pilot minimum)

### International
- Check local aviation authority regulations
- Most countries require registration and pilot certificate
- Some countries prohibit powered ultralights
