# PHI_GLIDER — Overview

## What It Is

An ultralight hang glider with phi-harmonic ducted fan motors for powered flight. The motors can provide thrust for takeoff and sustained flight, or be shut off for pure gliding. The wing uses phi-harmonic geometry (137.5° blade spacing) for maximum aerodynamic efficiency.

## What It Does

- Powered takeoff from flat ground (no hill needed)
- Sustained level flight at 30 km/h with motor on
- Pure gliding with motor off (8:1 glide ratio)
- Thermal soaring to gain altitude
- Emergency landing with motor restart
- Foldable wing for transport

## How It Works (Simplified)

1. **Phi-Harmonic Wing**: Wing struts use golden-angle bracing for optimal strength-to-weight
2. **Ducted Fan Motors**: 2× phi-harmonic ducted fans provide thrust for takeoff and climb
3. **Battery**: 36V Li-Ion provides 216 Wh for ~20 minutes powered flight
4. **Thermal Assist**: After motor off, pilot uses rising air currents to extend range
5. **Control**: Weight-shift control (standard hang glider technique)
