## Limitations

1. **Requires pilot training** — not a toy
2. **Weather dependent** — cannot fly in rain, fog, high wind
3. **Limited by weight** — pilots over 90 kg cannot fly safely
4. **Limited battery** — only 20 minutes powered flight
5. **No night flying** — without special equipment
6. **No instrument flying** — VFR only
7. **Landing zone required** — need 100m×50m clear area
