## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$900
- **Speed**: 30 km/h (18.6 mph) glide speed
- **Range**: 50 km (31 miles) with thermal assist
- **Weight Capacity**: 90 kg (198 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 2 hours
- **Motor**: 2× 200W phi-harmonic ducted fans

## What Makes It Special

An ultralight hang glider with phi-harmonic ducted fan motors mounted on the wing struts. The golden-angle blade arrangement reduces noise by 35% and increases thrust efficiency by 28%. When not under power, it glides like a standard hang glider with 8:1 glide ratio.
