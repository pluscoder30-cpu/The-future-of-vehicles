# PHI SUBMERSIBLE — README

## Quick Start
1. Launch submersible into water (crane or ramp)
2. Power on (hold button 3 sec)
3. Wait for self-test (2 min)
4. Board from rear hatch
5. Secure 2-point harness
6. Close and seal hatch
7. Engage field skimmer (toggle switch)
8. Use joysticks to navigate
9. Emergency ascent: Pull red handle

## Specifications at a Glance
- **MSRP:** $3,000

- **Max Depth:** 500m
- **Top Speed:** 25 km/h (submerged)
- **Range:** 24 hours / 600 km
- **Charge Time:** 30 min (phi-harmonic) / 4 hrs (standard)
- **Passengers:** 2

## Safety Warnings
- Always dive with a buddy (2-person capacity)
- Never exceed 500m rated depth
- Maintain 40% battery minimum for dive operations
- Test life support before every dive
- Practice emergency ascent before first deep dive

## Support
- Emergency: 433 MHz beacon (surface) / acoustic (underwater)
- Service: Authorized phi-harmonic service centers
- Updates: Over-the-air (surface only)
