# PHI SUBMERSIBLE — Overview

## Vehicle Identity
- **Designation:** PHI-SUB-001
- **Type:** Phi-Harmonic Underwater Field Skimmer
- **Class:** Personal Submersible
- **MSRP:** $3,000

## Core Concept
A personal submersible that uses phi-harmonic field skimming to glide through water with near-zero drag. The vehicle creates a phi-harmonic boundary layer that reduces water resistance by 98%, enabling underwater travel at speeds previously impossible for personal submersibles.

## Key Differentiators
- **Field Skimming:** 98% drag reduction via phi-harmonic boundary layer
- **Zero Friction:** Water flows around the phi-field envelope
- **Deep Dive:** Rated to 500m depth
- **Silent Operation:** No propeller noise (field propulsion)
- **Long Endurance:** 24 hours submerged

## Battery
- **Model:** FPB-20 (Field Plasma Battery — 20kWh)
- **Cost:** $3,000
- **Runtime:** 24 hours normal / 8 hours deep operations
- **Charge Time:** 30 minutes (phi-harmonic rapid)

## Primary Applications
- Underwater exploration and research
- Marine biology observation
- Underwater search and rescue
- Recreational diving platform
- Subsea infrastructure inspection

## Design Philosophy
Water resistance is the fundamental limit on underwater speed and efficiency. The Submersible eliminates drag not by streamlining (which only reduces it) but by creating a phi-harmonic field envelope that causes water molecules to flow around the vehicle without touching its surface. The vehicle literally skims through water on a cushion of coherent field energy.
