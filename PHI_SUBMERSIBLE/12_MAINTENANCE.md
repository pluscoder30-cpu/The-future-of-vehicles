# PHI SUBMERSIBLE — Maintenance Guide

## Maintenance Philosophy
The Submersible operates in a harsh saltwater environment. Corrosion prevention and seal integrity are the primary maintenance concerns.

## Maintenance Schedule

### Pre-Dive (Before Each Dive, 5 min)
- [ ] Visual hull inspection (above and below waterline)
- [ ] Field skimmer status check
- [ ] Battery level check (must be >40% for dive)
- [ ] Ballast system function test
- [ ] Life support check (O2 level, CO2 scrubber)
- [ ] Communication test (acoustic modem)
- [ ] Emergency beacon test

### After Each Dive
- [ ] Rinse hull with fresh water
- [ ] Inspect seals for debris
- [ ] Check viewport for scratches
- [ ] Log dive (depth, duration, battery usage)

### Monthly (3 hours)
- [ ] Full field coil resonance test
- [ ] Seal inspection and lubrication
- [ ] Hull integrity pressure test (surface)
- [ ] Ballast pump test
- [ ] Life support system test
- [ ] Acoustic modem calibration
- [ ] Battery full cycle test

### Annual (12 hours)
- [ ] Professional inspection at authorized service center
- [ ] Hull ultrasound thickness measurement
- [ ] Seal replacement (all O-rings)
- [ ] Viewport polish and inspection
- [ ] Field coil deep diagnostic
- [ ] Life support overhaul
- [ ] Ballast system overhaul
- [ ] Full system recertification
- [ ] Pressure test to 1500m

### Every 5 Years
- [ ] Complete hull re-certification
- [ ] Field coil refurbishment ($300)
- [ ] Viewport replacement (if needed)
- [ ] Complete seal kit replacement
- [ ] Life support system rebuild

## Storage
- Rinse thoroughly with fresh water after saltwater use
- Store in dry, temperature-controlled location
- Battery: store at 50% charge
- Hull: Cover with breathable fabric
- Viewport: Protect from UV and scratches
- Seals: Apply silicone lubricant before long-term storage
