# PHI SUBMERSIBLE — Quick Start Guide

## What You Built

A personal submersible with phi-harmonic vortex propulsion. It dives to 500m, runs 24 hours on battery, and is nearly silent underwater.

**Cost:** ~$1,400 | **Build Time:** 8-10 hours | **Weight:** ~45 kg

---

## Before You Dive

### Safety (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

1. **NEVER DIVE ALONE** — always have a buddy on the surface
2. Test kill switch — pull magnet, motor MUST stop in <1 second
3. Check O-ring seal — no debris, no nicks, lightly greased
4. Leak test — submerge empty hull 10 minutes, check for water inside
5. Battery charged — at least 80%
6. Dive flag visible from 100m+
7. O2 bottle accessible — within arm's reach
8. Buddy knows emergency procedures

### Pre-Dive Checklist

- [ ] O-ring inspected (item #5) — clean, greased, seated
- [ ] Dome seal tight (8 bolts, star pattern)
- [ ] Kill switch tested
- [ ] Battery >80%
- [ ] Propeller spins freely (no debris)
- [ ] All frame bolts tight
- [ ] Ballast pump tested (switch ON/OFF)
- [ ] Dive flag attached
- [ ] Safety gear inside (O2, knife, whistle, mirror)
- [ ] Surface marker buoy deployed

---

## Launch Sequence

1. **Place submersible in water** (calm area, waist-deep)
2. **Check for leaks** — wait 2 minutes, look inside
3. **Climb in** — sit comfortably, feet on frame rails
4. **Attach kill switch lanyard** to wrist
5. **Close dome** — verify seal
6. **Turn on battery monitor** — confirm >36V
7. **Turn on SW3** — lights and depth gauge on
8. **Turn on SW2** — ballast pump ready
9. **Turn on SW1** — motor armed (silent)
10. **Push throttle** — dive!

---

## Underwater Operations

### Controls

| Switch | Function |
|--------|----------|
| SW1 | Thruster ON/OFF |
| SW2 | Ballast pump ON/OFF |
| SW3 | Lights + depth gauge ON/OFF |
| Kill | Emergency motor stop |

### Dive Technique

1. **Surface:** Ballast tank empty, submersible floats
2. **Descend:** Turn SW2 ON (water fills ballast tank)
3. **Level off:** Turn SW2 OFF (pump stops filling)
4. **Ascend:** Ballast pump empties tank (auto or manual)
5. **Emergency surface:** Kill switch + ballast pump OUT

### Depth Management

- 0-30m: Recreational zone, easy
- 30-100m: Intermediate, check O2 supply
- 100-300m: Advanced, monitor battery carefully
- 300-500m: Expert only, check all seals before dive

---

## Emergency Procedures

### Leaking Water
1. Kill motor (pull magnet)
2. Ballast pump to maximum
3. Surface immediately
4. Bail water if pump can't keep up

### Motor Failure
1. Kill switch (if motor runs wild)
2. Use ballast to surface
3. Deploy surface marker buoy
4. Signal buddy

### Trapped/Tangled
1. Stay calm — conserve O2
2. Use dive knife to cut free
3. Ballast pump empty = float up
4. Signal buddy if needed

### O2 Depleted
1. Surface immediately
2. Deploy emergency O2 bottle
3. Signal buddy for assistance

---

## Surfacing Sequence

1. **Ballast pump to MAX** — empty tank fast
2. **Wait for buoyancy** — submersible rises
3. **Pop dome seal** — vent air at surface
4. **Signal buddy** — wave or mirror
5. **Climb out** — in calm water
6. **Tow to shore** — or use ballast to drift

---

## Post-Dive

1. **Rinse** — fresh water wash of entire sub
2. **Dry** — open dome, air dry 1 hour
3. **Check seals** — O-ring, flange, all gaskets
4. **Charge battery** — full charge after every dive
5. **Inspect prop** — no bent blades, no debris
6. **Log dive** — depth, time, battery usage
