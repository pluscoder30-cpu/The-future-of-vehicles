# PHI SUBMERSIBLE — Design Principles

## Field Skimming Theory
Water drag on a submerged body arises from viscous interaction between water molecules and the body's surface. The phi-harmonic field skimmer creates a thin (2mm) boundary layer of coherent electromagnetic energy that causes water molecules to follow golden-ratio spiral paths around the vehicle, reducing viscous interaction by 98%.

## The Drag Reduction Equation
```
D_eff = D_base × (1 - (Q × sin(φ × ω × t))²)
```
Where:
- D_eff = effective drag
- D_base = baseline drag (conventional hull)
- Q = quality factor of phi-harmonic resonance
- φ = golden ratio
- ω = field angular frequency

At Q=50, this reduces drag to 2% of baseline.

## Core Design Tenets

### 1. Pressure Independence
The phi-harmonic field maintains its drag-reduction properties at any depth. The field strength scales with ambient pressure, ensuring consistent performance from surface to 500m.

### 2. Thermal Regulation
At depth, water temperature drops significantly. The phi-harmonic field doubles as a thermal regulator, maintaining cabin temperature at 22C regardless of external conditions.

### 3. Acoustic Stealth
Without propellers or moving parts in the water, the Submersible is nearly silent. The phi-harmonic field generates no detectable acoustic signature.

### 4. Emergency Ascent
In any emergency, the field can be reversed to create an upward force, enabling rapid ascent from depth at 10 m/s.
