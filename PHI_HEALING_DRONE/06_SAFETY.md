| Condition | Safe to Use? | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Adult (18+) | Yes | Standard use |
| Child (5-17) | With doctor approval | Reduced frequency time |
| Child (under 5) | NO | Not recommended |

### Emergency Procedures

**If drone loses power in flight:**
1. Drone will auto-land (GPS-assisted)
2. Clear area below drone
3. Do NOT attempt to catch drone
4. Wait for complete shutdown before approaching

**If drone becomes unstable:**
1. Reduce throttle to minimum
2. Allow drone to land
3. Check for loose components
4. Recalibrate gyro/accelerometer

**If medication bay fails:**
1. Do NOT force open manually
2. Return drone to base
3. Open bay only after full shutdown
4. Inspect servo mechanisms

**If patient shows adverse reaction:**
1. Immediately stop frequency therapy
2. Land drone safely
3. Remove transducers from patient
4. Seek professional medical help
5. Document incident

**If fire occurs:**
1. Move away from drone
2. Do NOT use water on battery fire
3. Use fire extinguisher (CO2 or dry chemical)
4. Call emergency services
5. Ventilate area (lithium battery fumes are toxic)

## ENVIRONMENTAL SAFETY

### Weather Conditions

| Condition | Status | Action |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Clear, calm | Safe to fly | Proceed with mission |
| Light wind (<15 km/h) | Caution | Reduce altitude |

| Rain | DO NOT FLY | Abort mission |
| Snow | DO NOT FLY | Abort mission |

## STORAGE AND MAINTENANCE

### Storage

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Temperature | 10-30°C |
| Humidity | 30-70% RH |
| Battery | Store at 50% charge |
| Medications | Store separately, temperature controlled |
| Propellers | Remove for long-term storage |

### Maintenance Schedule

| Interval | Task |
|----------|------|
| Every flight | Visual inspection |
| Every 10 flights | Tighten all bolts |
| Every 25 flights | Calibrate sensors |
| Every 50 flights | Replace propellers |
| Every 100 flights | Full inspection |
| Every 6 months | Replace battery |
| Annually | Full rebuild recommended |
