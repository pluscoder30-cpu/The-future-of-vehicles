# PHI SYNTHETIC WINGS — Propulsion System

## Flight Propulsion
The Synthetic Wings generate lift and thrust through two mechanisms:

### Mechanism 1: Mechanical Flapping
- Wing motion driven by servo motors
- Mimics bird wing kinematics
- Generates baseline lift and thrust
- Controlled by wearer's arm movements

### Mechanism 2: Phi-Harmonic Lift Amplification
- Phi-harmonic field couples with wing motion
- Amplifies lift force by 100x
- Creates hovering capability
- Enables three-axis control
