# PHI SYNTHETIC WINGS — Overview

## Vehicle Identity
- **Designation:** PHI-SYNTH-WINGS-001
- **Type:** Biosynthetic Human Flight Wings
- **Class:** Personal Aerial Vehicle (Wearable)
- **MSRP:** $500

## Core Concept
Biosynthetic wings that attach to the human body, enabling sustained flight using phi-harmonic lift generation. The wings use a combination of biomimetic flapping mechanics and phi-harmonic field lift to generate enough upward force for a human to fly, hover, and maneuver in three dimensions.

## Key Differentiators
- **Human-Powered Flight:** Wings amplify human muscle output by 100x via phi-harmonic field
- **Biomimetic Design:** Flapping motion inspired by birds and bats
- **Hover Capability:** Station-keeping in mid-air
- **Three-Axis Control:** Pitch, yaw, and roll via body movement
- **Foldable:** Wings fold flat for ground transport

## Battery
- **Model:** FPB-5 (Field Plasma Battery — 5kWh)
- **Cost:** $500
- **Runtime:** 4 hours sustained flight
- **Charge Time:** 20 minutes (phi-harmonic rapid)

## Primary Applications
- Personal aerial transportation
- Search and rescue operations
- Aerial photography and videography
- Recreational flying
- Emergency evacuation
- Environmental monitoring

## Design Philosophy
Birds have been flying for 150 million years. The Synthetic Wings do not reinvent flight — they amplify what evolution already perfected. The phi-harmonic lift system takes the natural flapping motion of bird wings and amplifies the lift force by 100x through golden-ratio field coupling, making human flight not just possible but effortless.
