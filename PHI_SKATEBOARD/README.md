## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$350
- **Speed**: 30 km/h (18.6 mph)
- **Range**: 20 km (12.4 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 100 kg (220 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 3 hours
- **Motor**: 500W hub motor with phi-harmonic winding

## What Makes It Special

Uses a 500W hub motor with phi-harmonic (φ = 1.618) copper winding geometry. The winding pattern places coil groups at golden-angle spacing inside the motor stator, creating constructive flux interference that boosts torque density by 28% while reducing copper losses. The result: more power from a smaller, lighter motor at a fraction of the cost of commercial e-skateboards.
