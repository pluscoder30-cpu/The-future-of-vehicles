# PHI_SKATEBOARD — Safety Precautions

## CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS

> **READ ALL WARNINGS BEFORE OPERATING THE SKATEBOARD**
> **FAILURE TO FOLLOW SAFETY RULES CAN RESULT IN SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH**

---

## Electrical Safety

### Battery Hazards
- **LiFePO4 batteries store significant energy** — a short circuit can cause fire
- Never expose battery to temperatures above 60°C (140°F)
- Never puncture, crush, or disassemble battery cells
- If battery swells, smells, or smokes — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY** and move to open air
- Keep away from flammable materials during charging
- **Charge only with the supplied 36V LiFePO4 charger** — never use other chargers
- Charge in a well-ventilated area, not on beds or couches
- Unplug charger when fully charged

### High Voltage
- The battery operates at **36V DC** — this can cause burns
- Never touch battery terminals with bare hands
- Always disconnect battery before working on electronics
- Use insulated tools when working near live circuits

### Motor Heat
- Hub motor can reach temperatures up to 70°C during operation
- Never touch motor wheel immediately after riding
- Allow 15 minutes cool-down before handling

---

## Mechanical Safety

### Riding Risks
- **Always wear protective gear** — helmet, wrist guards, knee pads
- Start at low speed and gradually increase as you gain confidence
- Never ride faster than you can stop
- Keep feet on the deck at all times — never step off while moving
- Never ride in traffic, near pedestrians, or in enclosed spaces
- Maintain 3 meters of clearance from all obstacles
- Check surface for debris, water, oil, or cracks before each ride

### Speed Management
- Maximum speed: 30 km/h (18.6 mph) — this is fast enough to cause serious injury
- **Beginners**: Stay under 15 km/h until experienced
- Use regenerative braking — don't slam the brake
- On hills, reduce speed before the descent
- Never ride downhill at full speed

### Weight Limits
- Maximum rider weight: 100 kg (220 lbs)
- Exceeding weight limit reduces braking effectiveness and motor life
- Do not carry heavy backpacks while riding

## Emergency Procedures

### Board Malfunction
1. **Immediately let go of throttle**
2. Apply brakes gently
3. Step off the board when safe to do so
4. Move away from the board
5. If board is smoking or sparking, move at least 5 meters away

### Battery Emergency
1. If battery smokes or catches fire: **DO NOT USE WATER**
2. Move away from the area
3. Use a Class D fire extinguisher (metal fire) if available
4. Call emergency services (911)
5. If skin contact with battery acid, flush with water for 15 minutes

### Fall Response
1. If you fall, try to roll with the fall (don't extend arms)
2. Assess for injuries before standing
3. If you suspect a fracture, do not move the injured area
4. Call for help if needed

## Maintenance Safety

### Before Each Ride
- [ ] Check battery voltage (should be >33V)
- [ ] Check all bolts are tight
- [ ] Check motor wheel for damage or loose wires
- [ ] Check remote battery level
- [ ] Test throttle and brake at low speed
- [ ] Check surface for hazards

### Weekly Maintenance
- [ ] Clean motor wheel with dry cloth
- [ ] Check all wiring connections
- [ ] Test battery balance with multimeter
- [ ] Check wheel bearings for smoothness
- [ ] Tighten truck bolts if loose

### Monthly Maintenance
- [ ] Full battery charge cycle (discharge to 20%, charge to 100%)
- [ ] Check all solder joints for cracks
- [ ] Inspect deck for cracks or damage
- [ ] Clean and re-grip tape if worn
- [ ] Update ESC firmware if available
