# PHI BIO SKIN WATCH — Maintenance Guide

## Maintenance Philosophy
The Bio Skin Watch is designed to be maintenance-free. The self-cleaning bio-skin surface requires no cleaning, and the battery lasts up to a year.

## Maintenance Schedule

### Before First Use
- [ ] Charge battery (20 minutes on phi-harmonic dock)
- [ ] Download app and pair via Bluetooth
- [ ] Run initial calibration (5 minutes)

### Daily
- [ ] Nothing required (self-cleaning)

### Monthly
- [ ] Check battery level via app
- [ ] Update firmware if available
- [ ] Verify sensor accuracy (compare to medical device)

### Annually
- [ ] Charge battery (once per year)
- [ ] Check bio-skin integrity (no tears)
- [ ] Run full sensor calibration

### As Needed
- [ ] Wipe with damp cloth (optional, for peace of mind)
- [ ] Replace band if damaged (contact support)

## What NOT To Do
- Do NOT use soap, chemicals, or cleaners
- Do NOT scrub the surface
- Do NOT expose to extreme heat (>60C)
- Do NOT attempt to open the case
- Do NOT use abrasive materials

## Self-Cleaning in Action
The bio-skin cleans itself continuously:
- Water beads and rolls off instantly
- Fingerprints disappear in seconds
- Dust is repelled by electrostatic field
- Scratches heal within hours
- No user action required

## Battery Replacement
The battery is designed to last the lifetime of the watch (10+ years). If replacement is needed:
1. Contact authorized service center
2. Battery replacement: $50 (includes labor)
3. Bio-skin re-sealed during replacement

## Band Replacement
The bio-skin band is integrated and does not need replacement. If damaged:
1. Contact support
2. Band replacement: $25
3. Self-cleaning function maintained

## Storage
- Store on charger when not in use (optional)
- Battery: Maintains charge for 6+ months
- Temperature: -20C to +60C storage range
- No special packaging needed
