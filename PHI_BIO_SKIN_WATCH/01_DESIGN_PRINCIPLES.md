# PHI BIO SKIN WATCH — Design Principles

## Bio-Skin Theory
The Bio Skin Watch uses a living polymer surface that mimics biological self-cleaning mechanisms. The phi-harmonic surface pattern creates micro-structures that cause water and contaminants to bead and roll off (lotus effect), while the golden-ratio geometry ensures complete surface coverage with no dead zones.

## The Self-Cleaning Equation
```
C_clean(t) = C_base × (1 - e^(-k × t)) × (1 + Q × sin(φ × ω × t))
```
Where:
- C_clean = surface cleanliness
- C_base = base cleanliness (99.9%)
- k = cleaning rate constant
- φ = golden ratio
- Q = quality factor
- ω = phi-harmonic frequency

The surface achieves 99.9% cleanliness within 60 seconds of contamination.

## Core Design Tenets

### 1. Invisible Technology
The watch should feel like wearing nothing. No charging anxiety, no cleaning routine, no maintenance. It simply works.

### 2. Living Surface
The bio-skin is not a coating — it is a living polymer that actively responds to contamination. Dirt, oils, and water are repelled in real-time.

### 3. Self-Healing
Minor scratches in the bio-skin surface are repaired by the phi-harmonic field within hours. The surface literally grows back.

### 4. Adaptive Expression
The bio-skin can change color and texture on command, allowing the watch to match any outfit or mood.

## Aesthetic Language
- **Form:** Minimalist circle (38mm diameter)
- **Default Surface:** Matte black with golden phi-trace shimmer
- **Texture:** Smooth, organic, warm to touch
- **Display:** Hidden until needed (emerges from surface)
- **Band:** Integrated bio-skin (same material as face)
