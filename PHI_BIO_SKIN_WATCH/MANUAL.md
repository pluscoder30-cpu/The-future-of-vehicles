## 1. Introduction
The PHI Bio Skin Watch is a self-cleaning, self-healing wristwatch with a living bio-skin surface. It never needs cleaning, the battery lasts a year, and minor scratches heal themselves.

## 2. Getting Started
1. Remove from packaging
2. Charge battery: Place on phi-harmonic dock (20 minutes)
3. Download app: Search "Phi Bio Skin Watch"
4. Pair via Bluetooth: Open app, follow prompts
5. Run calibration: 5-minute guided process
6. Wear and enjoy

## 3. Wearing the Watch
1. Drape band around wrist
2. Magnetic phi-lock clasp snaps shut
3. Adjust for comfortable fit (not too tight)
4. Ensure heart rate sensor contacts skin
5. Raise wrist to see time

## 4. Telling Time
- **Raise to Wake:** Tilt wrist to see time
- **Double Tap:** Tap display twice to activate
- **Always-On:** Optional dim time display
- **Swipe:** Swipe to change watch face

## 5. Health Monitoring
- **Heart Rate:** Continuous or on-demand
- **SpO2:** Blood oxygen level
- **Temperature:** Skin temperature
- **Activity:** Steps, calories, distance
- **Sleep:** Sleep tracking and analysis

## 6. Notifications
- Phone calls, texts, and app alerts
- Swipe up to view, tap to dismiss
- Customize in app settings
- Do Not Disturb mode available

## 7. Contactless Payment
1. Set up in app (add card)
2. Double-press side button
3. Hold watch near payment terminal
4. Wait for confirmation
5. Works without phone nearby

## 8. Emergency Features
### Emergency SOS
1. Hold side button 5 seconds
2. Watch sends SOS to emergency contact
3. Includes GPS coordinates
4. Calls emergency services if no response

### Fall Detection
- Automatic fall detection
- Alerts emergency contact after 10 seconds
- Calls emergency services after 60 seconds

### Medical ID
- Hold both buttons to display
- Shows medical information for first responders

## 9. Self-Cleaning
The bio-skin surface cleans itself continuously:
- Water beads and rolls off
- Fingerprints disappear in seconds
- Dust is repelled automatically
- No cleaning required ever

## 10. Self-Healing
Minor scratches heal automatically:
- Detection: 0 seconds
- Healing: 2-8 hours
- No user action required
- Deep scratches: Contact service

## 11. Care and Maintenance
- **Do:** Wear daily, charge annually
- **Do Not:** Use soap, chemicals, or cleaners
- **Do Not:** Scrub or abrade the surface
- **Optional:** Wipe with damp cloth

## 12. Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| No display | Battery dead | Charge battery |
| Wrong time | Timezone issue | Update in app |
| No BLE | Phone out of range | Move closer |
| Sensor error | Contact issue | Adjust fit |
| Slow response | Low battery | Charge battery |
