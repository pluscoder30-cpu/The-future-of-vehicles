# PHI BIO SKIN WATCH — Overview

## Vehicle Identity
- **Designation:** PHI-BIO-SKIN-001
- **Type:** Self-Cleaning Bio Skin Wristwatch
- **Class:** Personal Wearable Device
- **MSRP:** $100

## Core Concept
A wristwatch with a living bio-skin surface that self-cleans, self-repairs, and adapts its appearance to the wearer's style. The phi-harmonic bio-skin uses golden-ratio patterns to maintain a continuously fresh, clean surface that sheds dirt, repels water, and regenerates from minor scratches.

## Key Differentiators
- **Self-Cleaning:** Phi-harmonic surface repels all contaminants
- **Self-Repairing:** Minor scratches heal within hours
- **Living Surface:** Bio-skin maintains its own cleanliness
- **Adaptive Appearance:** Changes texture and color on command
- **Zero Maintenance:** No cleaning, no polishing, no care

## Battery
- **Model:** FPB-5 (Field Plasma Battery — 5kWh)
- **Cost:** $100
- **Runtime:** 365 days (1 year)
- **Charge Time:** 20 minutes (phi-harmonic rapid)

## Primary Applications
- Personal timekeeping
- Health monitoring (heart rate, SpO2, temperature)
- Style expression (color/texture changes)
- Notification display
- Contactless payment
- Emergency beacon

## Design Philosophy
The Bio Skin Watch embodies the principle that technology should be invisible. The living surface maintains itself, the battery lasts a year, and the interface disappears when not needed. It is the most low-maintenance high-technology device ever created.
