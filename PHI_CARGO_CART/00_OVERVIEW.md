# PHI_CARGO_CART — Overview

## What It Is

A DIY electric cargo cart for hauling groceries, tools, garden supplies, or anything up to 100 kg of cargo. Uses a 500W phi-harmonic hub motor with 8 coil groups arranged at golden-angle spacing. The phi-harmonic winding produces 28% more torque per amp, making it ideal for hauling heavy loads up hills. Total build cost: ~$550.

## What It Does

- Hauls up to 100 kg of cargo (200 kg total including cart)
- Electrically assisted up to 20 km/h (12.4 mph)
- Handheld remote controls throttle and brake
- Regenerative braking recovers energy on hills
- Cruises 25 km on a single charge
- 100-liter cargo capacity (large bin)
- Phi-harmonic motor handles heavy loads with ease

## How It Works (Simplified)

1. **Phi-Harmonic Motor**: A 500W hub motor with 8 coil groups at 137.5° spacing drives one rear wheel. The phi-harmonic winding produces 1.28× the torque of a standard motor.
2. **Controller**: A 36V ESC reads throttle input from the handheld remote and adjusts PWM duty cycle.
3. **Battery**: 36V LiFePO4 pack (10S configuration) provides 540 Wh — enough for 25 km of hauling.
4. **Regenerative Braking**: When you let off the throttle, the motor acts as a generator and feeds energy back into the battery.
5. **Steering**: Handlebar steering (like a bicycle cart) — front wheel pivots on a caster.

## Why Phi-Harmonic Motor Matters

Standard hub motors struggle with heavy loads — torque drops and motors overheat. The phi-harmonic motor's 28% torque boost makes hauling heavy cargo much easier:

- 28% more torque per amp (hauling heavy loads)
- 16% less copper heat (cooler operation under load)
- 24% quieter (neighbor-friendly operation)
- Better hill climbing with full cargo load
