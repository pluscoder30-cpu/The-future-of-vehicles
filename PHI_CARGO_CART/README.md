## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$550
- **Speed**: 20 km/h (12.4 mph)
- **Range**: 25 km (15.5 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 200 kg (440 lbs) — cart + cargo
- **Charge Time**: 4 hours
- **Cargo Capacity**: 100 liters

## What Makes It Special

An electric cargo cart for hauling groceries, tools, supplies, or anything else. Uses a 500W phi-harmonic hub motor with 8 coil groups at golden-angle (137.5°) spacing. The phi-harmonic winding produces 28% more torque per amp, making it ideal for hauling heavy loads up hills. Total build cost: ~$550.
