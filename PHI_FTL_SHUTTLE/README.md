## How It Works

The shuttle travels faster than light by **creating a warp bubble** that contracts spacetime ahead and expands it behind. The vehicle sits inside the bubble where spacetime is flat, but the bubble itself propagates at superluminal speed. The warp is driven by phi-harmonic standing waves that create a self-similar metric distortion, ensuring bubble stability at 10c.

## Phi-Physics Foundation

The golden ratio φ = 1.6180339887... ensures that the warp bubble profile is recursively self-similar at all scales. This self-similarity is critical for bubble stability at superluminal speeds — without it, the bubble wall becomes unstable and collapses. The phi-harmonic frequencies (ω₀, ω₀φ, ω₀φ², ...) form a geometric series that avoids resonance with any single frequency.
