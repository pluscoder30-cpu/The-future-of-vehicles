# PHI_FTL_SHUTTLE — Faster-Than-Light Warp Drive Spacecraft

## Classification
- **Class**: PHI FTL Shuttle (FTL-1)
- **Type**: Phi-harmonic warp drive vehicle
- **Manufacturer**: PHI Physics Division
- **Status**: Prototype — flight-tested in controlled deep-space conditions

## Summary

The PHI_FTL_SHUTTLE is a faster-than-light spacecraft that achieves superluminal travel by contracting spacetime ahead of the vehicle and expanding it behind, creating a warp bubble that carries the vehicle at 10× the speed of light. The warp is driven by phi-harmonic standing waves that generate a self-similar metric distortion, allowing the bubble to remain stable at speeds far exceeding c.

Unlike the teleport shuttle (which folds spatial dimensions), the FTL shuttle moves through normal spacetime but modifies the metric so that the effective velocity exceeds light speed. The vehicle itself never exceeds c locally — it is the warp bubble that propagates at superluminal speed.

## Operating Principle

FTL travel is achieved through **warp drive** — a metric engineering technique where spacetime is contracted ahead of the vehicle and expanded behind it. The vehicle sits inside a **warp bubble** where spacetime is flat, but the bubble itself propagates through spacetime at superluminal speed.

The warp is driven by **phi-harmonic standing waves** that create a self-similar metric distortion. The golden ratio φ = 1.6180339887... ensures that the warp bubble profile is recursively self-similar at all scales, which is necessary for bubble stability at superluminal speeds.
