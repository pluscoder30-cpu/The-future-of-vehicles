## Cost-Saving Tips

1. **Salvage components** from old electronics (saves 70-80%)
2. **Buy from AliExpress** instead of Amazon (saves 40-50%)
3. **Join a buying group** with friends (saves 30-40%)
4. **Build MVB** (Minimum Viable Battery) for 80-90% less
5. **Use fiberglass** instead of aerogel (saves 15-25%)
