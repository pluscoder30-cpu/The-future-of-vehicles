## Step 2: Connect Power (1 minute)

```
POWER CONNECTION:

XT90 CONNECTOR:
  ┌──────┐      ┌──────┐
  │  +   │      │  -   │
  │(Red) │      │(Black)│
  └──────┘      └──────┘
   48V DC        GND

1. Match red wire to +, black wire to -
2. Push XT90 connectors together until they click
3. Do NOT force — they should fit easily

⚠️ WRONG POLARITY = DAMAGE! Double-check before connecting!
```

---

## Step 3: Power On (1 minute)

```
POWER ON PROCEDURE:

1. Connect power source (48V DC)
2. Status LED should blink green
3. MCU boots in <2 seconds
4. All 5 coils activate (you may hear a faint hum)
5. Battery is ready to use!

If LED is red: Check connections, power cycle.
```

## Charging Options

### Self-Charging (Automatic)
- Battery charges itself from vibrations, heat, EMF
- No action required — charging is automatic!
- Rate: 20-600W continuous

### External Charging (Optional)
1. Connect 48V DC source to XT90 input
2. Red = positive, Black = negative
3. Charge rate: Up to 20-200A
4. Time: 1-4 hours (depending on source)

### Solar Charging (Optional)
1. Connect 48V solar panel array
2. MPPT controller optimizes charging
3. Minimum 100W for FPB-5
4. Minimum 500W for FPB-40+

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| No power output | Coil power off | Check 48V supply, reset MCU |
| Low power output | Plasma depleted | Refill gas (H₂/He mix) |
| Overheating | Ambient >80°C | Move to cooler location |
| Gas leak sound | Seal failure | Replace O-rings, refill gas |
| Erratic output | MCU glitch | Power cycle battery |
| No self-charging | Low ambient energy | Move to higher energy area |

## Connectors Pinout

```
XT90 POWER CONNECTOR:
  + (Red) = 48V DC
  - (Black) = GND

JST-SH DIAGNOSTIC (4-pin):
  Pin 1: 3.3V (sensor power)
  Pin 2: SDA (I2C data)
  Pin 3: SCL (I2C clock)
  Pin 4: GND
```
