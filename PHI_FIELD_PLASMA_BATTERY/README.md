## The Safest Battery Ever Made

The PHI-HARMONIC FIELD PLASMA BATTERY (FPB) is a next-generation energy storage device that uses **plasma** instead of chemicals. It's inherently safer than lithium-ion — if containment fails, plasma dissipates harmlessly within milliseconds.

## Safety Summary

The FPB is **inherently safe** by physics:

- **Zero fire risk** — plasma cannot burn
- **Zero explosion risk** — plasma dissipates when containment fails
- **Zero toxic fumes** — hydrogen and helium are non-toxic
- **Zero thermal runaway** — no chemical reactions
- **Containment failure** — plasma safe in <10 milliseconds
