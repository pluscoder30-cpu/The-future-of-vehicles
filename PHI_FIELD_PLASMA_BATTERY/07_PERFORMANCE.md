## Self-Charging Rates

### What is Self-Charging?

The FPB battery can **charge itself** from energy in the environment! It captures:
- **Vibrations** from driving (piezoelectric elements)
- **Heat** from brakes and engine (thermoelectric modules)
- **Electromagnetic waves** from the motor (RF coils)
- **Sunlight** (if solar panels are added)

### Self-Charging by Vehicle Type

| Vehicle | Harvesting Rate | Daily Harvest | % of Capacity |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|---------------|
| E-bike | 15 W | 0.24 kWh | 4.8% |
| Hover car | 50 W | 0.80 kWh | 8.0% |
| Electric truck | 80 W | 1.28 kWh | 6.4% |
| Electric bus | 120 W | 1.92 kWh | 4.8% |
| Electric plane | 200 W | 3.20 kWh | 8.0% |
| Spacecraft | 300 W | 4.80 kWh | 6.0% |

**Note:** Self-charging extends range but does not replace primary charging. It provides 5-10% additional range per day.
