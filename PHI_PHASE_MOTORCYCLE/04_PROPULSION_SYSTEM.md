# PHI PHASE MOTORCYCLE — Propulsion System

## Dual-Mode Propulsion
The Phase Motorcycle operates in two distinct modes:

### Mode 1: Normal Riding
- Electric hub motor drives rear wheel
- Conventional tire contact with road
- Zero emissions, near-silent operation
- Top speed: 120 km/h
- Range: 200 km

### Mode 2: Phase-Shifting
- Phase coils engage, motorcycle enters phase state
- Tires lose contact with road surface (floating)
- Motorcycle passes through solid barriers
- Top speed (phased): 60 km/h
- Range (phased): 45 minutes / 45 km

## Phase Shift Sequence
1. **Preparation** (2 sec): Coils power up, rider notified
2. **Synchronization** (0.5 sec): Vehicle speed matched to phase windows
3. **Phase Engage** (variable): Coils modulate at golden ratio frequency
4. **Transit** (variable): Motorcycle passes through barrier
5. **Phase Disengage** (0.5 sec): Coils power down, tires re-engage road
6. **Recovery** (1 sec): Systems normalize, rider notified
