# PHI PHASE MOTORCYCLE — Overview

## Vehicle Identity
- **Designation:** PHI-PHASE-MOTO-001
- **Type:** Phase-Shifting Motorcycle
- **Class:** Personal Ground Vehicle
- **MSRP:** $800

## Core Concept
A two-wheeled motorcycle capable of shifting its molecular phase state, allowing it to pass through thin solid objects (walls, fences, barriers up to 30cm thick). The phase shift is achieved through phi-harmonic phase coils that temporarily reduce the vehicle's interaction cross-section with normal matter.

## Key Differentiators
- **Phase Shift:** Passes through solid barriers up to 30cm thick
- **Zero Contact:** No friction, no wear on tires or road surface
- **Phi-Harmonic Phase Coils:** Golden-ratio field geometry enables coherent phase transition
- **Stealth Operation:** Near-silent in phase mode
- **Dual Mode:** Normal riding + phase shift capability

## Battery
- **Model:** FPB-10 (Field Plasma Battery — 10kWh)
- **Cost:** $800
- **Runtime:** 12 hours normal / 45 minutes phase-shifted
- **Charge Time:** 25 minutes (phi-harmonic rapid)

## Primary Applications
- Emergency vehicle bypass (ambulance, fire)
- Security and law enforcement access
- Personal transportation through urban barriers
- Search and rescue in collapsed structures
- Recreational phase-riding

## Design Philosophy
Phase shifting is not teleportation — it is the controlled reduction of a vehicle's interaction with the electromagnetic force that prevents solid objects from occupying the same space. By oscillating the vehicle's electron cloud at the golden ratio frequency, the phi-harmonic coils create windows of reduced interaction, allowing passage through barriers.
