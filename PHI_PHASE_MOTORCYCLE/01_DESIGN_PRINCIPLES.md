# PHI PHASE MOTORCYCLE — Design Principles

## Phase Shift Theory
Matter appears solid because electron clouds in adjacent atoms repel each other via the electromagnetic force. The phi-harmonic phase coils generate a field that modulates this interaction. At the golden ratio frequency, the modulation creates a coherent "phase window" where the repulsive force drops below the threshold needed to prevent passage.

## The Phase Equation
```
P_phase = P_base × (1 - cos(φ × ω × t)) / 2
```
Where:
- P_phase = phase penetration factor (0 = solid, 1 = fully phased)
- P_base = base phase capability (determined by coil power)
- φ = golden ratio
- ω = phase coil angular frequency
- t = time

## Core Design Tenets

### 1. Controlled Vulnerability
During phase shift, the vehicle is partially immune to physical barriers but also partially immune to physical support. The system must balance penetration with structural integrity.

### 2. Temporal Precision
Phase windows occur in microsecond bursts. The onboard computer must synchronize the vehicle's motion with the phase windows to ensure complete passage before the phase window closes.

### 3. Rider Safety
The rider experiences no physical sensation during phase shift — the coils phase both vehicle and rider simultaneously. Safety harness prevents separation during the transition.

### 4. Energy Efficiency
Phase shifting is energy-intensive. The phi-harmonic geometry reduces the energy cost of each phase window by 60% compared to linear frequency approaches.

## Aesthetic Language
- **Form:** Aggressive sport-bike geometry with visible phase coil arrays
- **Color:** Deep black with iridescent phase-shift shimmer when active
- **Texture:** Matte carbon fiber with golden φ-trace inlays
- **Illumination:** Phase coils glow golden during shift preparation
