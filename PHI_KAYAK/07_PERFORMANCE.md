## Limitations

1. **Requires calm water** — not for ocean or rapids
2. **Limited by weight** — heavy loads reduce speed significantly
3. **Weather dependent** — cannot paddle in storms
4. **Charging time** — 2.5 hours for full charge
5. **No reverse** — must paddle backward manually
6. **Draft** — requires minimum 30cm water depth
