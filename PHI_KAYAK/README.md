## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$500
- **Speed**: 15 km/h (9.3 mph)
- **Range**: 30 km (18.6 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 150 kg (330 lbs) total (kayak + person + gear)
- **Charge Time**: 2.5 hours
- **Motor**: 200W phi-harmonic water jet

## What Makes It Special

A recreational kayak with a 200W phi-harmonic water jet motor for assisted propulsion. The motor uses golden-angle (137.5°) impeller blade spacing for silent operation and maximum efficiency. When not under power, it paddles like a standard kayak.
