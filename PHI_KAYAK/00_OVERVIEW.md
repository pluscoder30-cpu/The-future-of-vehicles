# PHI_KAYAK — Overview

## What It Is

A recreational sit-on-top kayak with a 200W phi-harmonic water jet motor for assisted propulsion. The motor is mounted under the stern and provides thrust for cruising, fishing, or fighting currents. When not under power, it paddles like a standard kayak.

## What It Does

- Electric propulsion up to 15 km/h (9.3 mph)
- Thumb throttle for motor control
- 3 speed modes (Slow, Medium, Fast)
- Regenerative braking recovers energy when coasting
- Waterproof electronics compartment
- Paddle-activated safety switch

## How It Works (Simplified)

1. **Water Jet Motor**: Impeller draws water in through intake, accelerates it, and expels it out the back
2. **Phi-Harmonic Impeller**: Golden-angle blade arrangement for silent operation
3. **Battery**: 24V LiFePO4 provides 240 Wh capacity
4. **Throttle**: Thumb throttle controls motor speed
5. **Paddle Switch**: Safety switch activates only when paddle is inserted
