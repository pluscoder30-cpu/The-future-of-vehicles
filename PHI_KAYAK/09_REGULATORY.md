# PHI_KAYAK — Regulatory Status

## Legal Classification

The phi-harmonic kayak is classified as a **motorized kayak** or **electric kayak** in most jurisdictions. Here's how it fits into existing laws:

### Federal Classification (United States)

**1. Not a "Motor Vessel"**
- Under 46 CFR, a "motor vessel" requires more than 10 hp
- Our motor: 200W = 0.27 hp — **well under 10 hp**
- **Does not require USCG vessel documentation**
- **Does not require state registration** in most states

**2. State-Level Classification**
Most states treat motorized kayaks similarly to canoes/kayaks:

## Why It's Legal

### 1. Under 10 HP Limit
- 200W = 0.27 hp — **well under 10 hp limit**
- Most states exempt motors under 10 hp from registration
- No vessel documentation required
- No USCG inspection required

### 2. No Registration Required
- Most states don't require registration for motors under 10 hp
- No license plate needed
- Just launch and paddle

### 3. No Insurance Required
- Most states don't require insurance for kayaks
- **Recommended** but not required
- Homeowner's insurance may cover liability

### 4. No Special Parking
- Can park at any kayak launch
- Can transport on car roof rack
- Can store in garage or shed

## How to Stay Legal

### Do's
✅ Wear PFD (required by law in all states)
✅ Carry whistle (required on boats in most states)
✅ Use navigation light after sunset
✅ Follow speed limits (no-wake zones)
✅ Yield to larger vessels
✅ Respect wildlife and environment

### Don'ts
❌ Don't paddle under the influence of alcohol or drugs
❌ Don't paddle without PFD
❌ Don't exceed no-wake speed in restricted areas
❌ Don't block boat channels
❌ Don't disturb wildlife nests or habitats
❌ Don't dump trash or waste in water

## Phi-Harmonic Motor: Legal Nuance

The phi-harmonic motor arrangement is **functionally identical** to a standard electric motor for legal purposes:

- Same power rating (200W = 0.27 hp)
- Same classification (motorized kayak)
- Same registration requirements (none)

The phi-harmonic geometry is an **internal design improvement** — it doesn't change the legal classification. It's like using better bearings in a kayak motor — still just a kayak.
