# PHI_CHEAP_MEDIUM_PLANE — Project Overview

## Classification
**Twin-Engine Phi-Harmonic Light Transport — Commercial Ultralight**

## Mission Statement
Design and document a 4-6 seat twin-engine aircraft powered by phi-harmonic field batteries, achieving commercial ultralight classification at a total build cost under $8,000. The aircraft bridges experimental amateur-built and Part 103 ultralight categories, enabling practical short-haul transport.

## Design Philosophy

### Why Twin-Engine?
- Redundancy: single-engine failure still provides sufficient power for controlled descent
- Lower per-motor stress: each motor runs at 30 kW instead of one at 60 kW
- Balanced thrust: reduces asymmetric yaw on single-engine operation
- Two smaller propellers are quieter than one large propeller

### Why $7,500?
- Under FAA Part 103 value threshold for ultralight vehicles
- Affordable for experimental/amateur builders
- Uses commercially available components
- No custom-machined parts required
- Designed for home workshop construction

### Phi-Harmonic Integration
- FPB-40 phi-harmonic field plasma batteries use phi-harmonic resonance coupling (φ = 1.618034)
- Propellers tuned to phi-harmonic frequency ratio for minimum vibration
- Structural resonance avoidance at φ-multiple frequencies
- Field battery chemistry: Phi-harmonic field plasma (hydrogen confinement) — Zero fire/explosion risk — plasma is self-limiting

## Classification Path
- **Primary**: FAA Part 103 Ultralight (if under 317 kg / 70 lb empty — NOT applicable at 800 kg)
- **Secondary**: Experimental Amateur-Built (FAR Part 103 exemption unlikely)
- **Actual**: Experimental — Amateur Built (FAR Part 21.191)
  - Requires airworthiness certificate
  - Requires Licensed A&P for final inspection
  - Operating limitations issued by FSDO
  - No passenger restrictions if type-certified

## Key Innovations
1. **Phi-Harmonic Battery System**: 8× FPB-40 phi-harmonic field plasma batteries in phi-lattice array — Zero fire/explosion risk — plasma is self-limiting
2. **Resonance-Tuned Propellers**: Blade pass frequency aligned to φ-harmonic nodes
3. **Compliance-Optimized Structure**: Aluminum tube frame meets EAA standards
4. **Redundant Power**: Twin motors with independent ESC and battery groups
5. **Modular Wing Design**: Bolt-on wings for transport/storage
