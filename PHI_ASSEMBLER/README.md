**Build anything at burger price.**

This is not a 3D printer. It does not add material layer by layer. It does not melt, extrude, or sinter. **It tunes a standing wave field to the phi-harmonic resonance of the target molecular structure, then places loose feedstock material inside the field. The molecules self-organize into the target geometry — guided by the phi-harmonic carrier recursion.**
