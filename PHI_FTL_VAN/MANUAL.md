## What Is the PHI FTL Van?

The PHI FTL Van is a super cool cargo van that can travel to different dimensions! It uses a special battery called the FPB-80 that creates a magic bubble around the van, letting it go faster than light! It costs $14,000 to build.

### How Does It Travel to Other Dimensions?

1. **BATTERY** — The FPB-80 battery creates special sound waves
2. **WARP FIELD** — These sound waves bend space around the van
3. **DIMENSION** — The van slips through a portal to another dimension!

## How to Jump to Another Dimension

```
STEP-BY-STEP:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Load and secure cargo
  2. Check battery (need at least 20%)
  3. Pick a dimension (D0-D6)
  4. Press the dimension button
  5. Press JUMP
  6. Wait 2.2 seconds (count: 1... 2!)
  7. You're in the new dimension!
  
  To come home: Press HOME button
  
  Emergency: Press the big RED RETURN button
```
