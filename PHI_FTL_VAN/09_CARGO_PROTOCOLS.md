## LOADING PROCEDURE

```
CARGO LOADING CHECKLIST:
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. INSPECT CARGO AREA
     □ Floor clear of debris
     □ Tie-down points accessible
     □ Cargo lights functional
     □ Temperature acceptable

  2. LOAD CARGO
     □ Heaviest items first (floor level)
     □ Distribute weight evenly
     □ Leave access to emergency exits
     □ Secure with phi-locks

  3. VERIFY LOAD
     □ Total weight < 6,000 kg
     □ Weight distribution 40/60 (front/rear)
     □ No items blocking doors
     □ All tie-downs engaged

  4. PRE-JUMP CHECK
     □ Cargo shift sensor: GREEN
     □ Load balance: GREEN
     □ Door locks: ENGAGED
     □ Emergency release: ACCESSIBLE
```
