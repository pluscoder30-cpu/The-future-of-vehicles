## How to Make Gold

```
STEP-BY-STEP:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Make sure the machine is plugged in
  2. Put copper powder in the top hopper
  3. Close the hopper lid
  4. Check the display — it should say "READY"
  5. Press the green START button
  6. Watch the status ring:
     - Blue pulse = warming up (15 minutes)
     - Green solid = making gold!
  7. Wait 2-4 hours (it's working!)
  8. Watch the display for gold amount
  9. When done, press STOP
  10. Wait 30 minutes (cooling down)
  11. Open the bottom tray
  12. Collect your GOLD!

  That's it! Real 24K gold!
```
