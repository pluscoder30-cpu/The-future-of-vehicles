## WHAT IS THE PHI AI TOXIC DRONE?

The PHI AI Toxic Drone is a flying robot with AI that cleans up toxic waste! It uses sensors to identify dangerous chemicals, AI to figure out how to neutralize them, and sprays the right neutralizer to make them safe. It can work with other drones to clean up big spills!

### How Does It Clean Up Toxins?

The drone has FOUR ways to help:

1. **FINDS TOXINS** — Sensors detect dangerous chemicals
2. **IDENTIFIES TYPE** — AI figures out what the chemical is
3. **PLANS CLEANUP** — AI figures out how to neutralize it
4. **CLEANS UP** — Sprays the right neutralizer

## How to Use the AI Features

### Starting a Hazmat Mission

```
AI HAZMAT MISSION:
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Fill neutralizer tank (1.5L)
  2. Choose neutralizer type for expected toxin
  3. Charge battery fully
  4. Turn on drone
  5. Wait for AI to boot
  6. Connect phone to WiFi
  7. Open drone app
  8. Press "AI APPROACH"
  9. AI scans from safe distance
  10. AI identifies toxin type
  11. AI recommends cleanup plan
  12. You approve or modify
  13. AI cleans up the spill
```
