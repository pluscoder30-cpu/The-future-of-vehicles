## AI CAPABILITIES

### 1. Toxin Identification
- AI analyzes sensor patterns to identify specific chemicals
- Classifies toxin type (acid, base, organic, heavy metal)
- Estimates concentration levels
- Predicts health risks

### 2. Neutralization Planning
- AI calculates optimal neutralizer type and amount
- Plans application sequence
- Predicts neutralization completion time
- Monitors chemical reaction progress

### 3. Cleanup Coordination
- AI coordinates multiple hazmat drones
- Assigns decontamination zones
- Tracks cleanup progress
- Generates hazmat incident reports
