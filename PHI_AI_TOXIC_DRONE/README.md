# PHI AI TOXIC DRONE

## AI-Powered Toxic Waste Cleanup Drone

**$498 - FPB-10 Battery - AI Toxin Identification - Neutralization Planning - 100% DIY**

---

## WHAT IS THIS?

The PHI AI Toxic Drone is a dirt-cheap hazmat drone with AI capabilities you can build for under $500. It identifies toxins, plans neutralization, and coordinates cleanup with other drones.

## AI FEATURES

### Toxin Identification
- AI analyzes sensor patterns to identify chemicals
- Classifies toxin type (acid, base, organic, heavy metal)
- Estimates concentration and risk level

### Neutralization Planning
- AI calculates optimal neutralizer type and amount
- Plans application sequence and pattern
- Monitors reaction progress

### Cleanup Coordination
- AI coordinates multiple hazmat drones
- Assigns decontamination zones
- Generates hazmat incident reports
