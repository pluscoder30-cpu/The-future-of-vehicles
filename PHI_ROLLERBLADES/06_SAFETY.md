# PHI_ROLLERBLADES — Safety Precautions

## CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS

> **READ ALL WARNINGS BEFORE OPERATING THE ROLLERBLADES**
> **FAILURE TO FOLLOW SAFETY RULES CAN RESULT IN SERIOUS INJURY OR DEATH**

---

## Electrical Safety

### Battery Hazards
- **LiFePO4 batteries store significant energy** — short circuit can cause fire
- Never expose battery to temperatures above 60°C (140°F)
- Never puncture, crush, or disassemble battery cells
- If battery swells, smells, or smokes — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY**
- Keep battery pouches dry — water damages batteries
- **Charge only with the supplied 36V LiFePO4 charger**
- Remove battery pouches before charging
- Never charge batteries while wearing the skates

### Motor Heat
- Hub motors can reach temperatures up to 60°C during extended use
- Never touch motor immediately after riding
- Allow 10 minutes cool-down before handling

---

## Riding Safety

### Balance & Coordination
- **You MUST know how to rollerblade before adding electric boost**
- Practice on regular rollerblades first (without motors)
- Start at lowest throttle setting
- Never use electric boost until comfortable skating without it
- Keep knees bent, weight centered
- Never lean too far forward (motor will accelerate you)

### Speed Management
- Maximum speed: 25 km/h (15.5 mph) — this is fast for rollerblades
- **Beginners**: Stay under 10 km/h until experienced
- Use heel brake for stopping — practice braking before riding fast
- Never brake suddenly — progressive braking prevents falls
- Reduce speed before turns

### Terrain
- **ONLY use on smooth, flat surfaces** (pavement, concrete)
- Never use on rough terrain, grass, gravel, or wet surfaces
- Check surface for cracks, debris, or obstacles before each use
- Avoid downhill slopes until experienced

## Emergency Procedures

### Fall Response
1. If you feel yourself falling, try to bend knees and lower center of gravity
2. Fall sideways if possible (don't extend arms)
3. Slide on knee pads if necessary
4. Assess for injuries before standing
5. If you suspect a fracture, do not move the injured area

### Motor Malfunction
1. **Immediately lean back** (reduces throttle to zero)
2. Apply heel brake to stop
3. Step out of skates if possible
4. Turn off power switches
5. Check for issues before continuing

### Battery Emergency
1. If battery smokes or catches fire: **DO NOT USE WATER**
2. Remove battery pouch immediately (if safe to do so)
3. Move away from the area
4. Use Class D fire extinguisher if available
5. Call emergency services if needed

## Maintenance Safety

### Before Each Ride
- [ ] Check battery charge (should be >30V)
- [ ] Check all wheels spin freely
- [ ] Check brake functions
- [ ] Check lean sensor activates motor
- [ ] Check Bluetooth sync works
- [ ] Check surface for hazards

### Weekly Maintenance
- [ ] Check wheel bearings for smoothness
- [ ] Check all bolts are tight
- [ ] Check battery balance
- [ ] Clean motor wheels
- [ ] Check FSR sensor response

### Monthly Maintenance
- [ ] Full battery charge cycle
- [ ] Check all solder joints
- [ ] Inspect boots for cracks
