## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$450
- **Speed**: 25 km/h (15.5 mph)
- **Range**: 15 km (9.3 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 90 kg (198 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 2.5 hours
- **Motor**: 200W per boot (400W total)

## What Makes It Special

Electric rollerblades with phi-harmonic wheel geometry. Each boot has a 200W hub motor with 8 coil groups arranged at golden-angle (137.5°) spacing. The phi-harmonic winding produces 28% more torque per amp, giving better acceleration and hill climbing. Total build cost: ~$450 — less than commercial electric skates.
