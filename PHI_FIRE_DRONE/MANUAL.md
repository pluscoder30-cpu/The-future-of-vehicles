# PHI FIRE DRONE — Kid-Friendly Instruction Manual

## What Is the PHI Fire Drone?

The PHI Fire Drone is a flying robot that helps find and stop forest fires! It has a special heat camera that can see fire from far away, and it carries water or fire retardant to put out small fires. It costs less than $400 to build!

### How Does It Fight Fires?

1. **DETECTS** — Heat camera sees fire from 100 meters away
2. **ALERTS** — Tells fire department about the fire
3. **SUPPLIES** — Drops water or retardant on small fires
