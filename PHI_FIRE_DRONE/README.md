# PHI FIRE DRONE

## Forest Fire Detection and Suppression Drone

**$400 - FPB-10 Battery - Phi-Harmonic Heat Detection - 100% DIY**

---

## WHAT IS THIS?

The PHI Fire Drone detects forest fires early using phi-harmonic heat detection and suppresses small fires with fire retardant. It patrols forests autonomously, detects heat signatures with thermal cameras, and can drop fire retardant on small fires before they spread.

**Cost: $400 | Weight: 2.8 kg | Flight Time: 3 hours**

## SAFETY RATING
