## FIRE DETECTION THRESHOLDS

| Temperature | Alert Level | Action |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| < 50°C | Normal | Continue patrol |
| 50-100°C | Warning | Alert operator |
| 100-200°C | High | Investigate |
| > 200°C | Critical | Alert fire department |
| Smoke detected | Warning | Investigate immediately |

---

## RETARDANT USE

| Fire Size | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| < 0.5m² | Apply retardant |
| 0.5-1m² | Apply retardant + alert |
| 1-5m² | Alert fire department |
| > 5m² | DO NOT approach, alert only |
