## WHAT IS THIS?

The PHI Water Drone cleans rivers, lakes, and ponds using phi-harmonic filtration frequencies. It hovers over water surfaces, filters pollutants, removes algae, and applies frequencies that promote water purification.

**Cost: $280 | Weight: 2.2 kg | Flight Time: 3.5 hours**
