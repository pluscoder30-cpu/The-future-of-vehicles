## What Is the PHI Water Drone?

### How Does It Clean Water?

1. **FILTERS** — Three filters remove dirt, chemicals, and germs
2. **TESTS** — Sensors check if water is clean
3. **PLAYS SOUNDS** — Special sounds help water stay clean
