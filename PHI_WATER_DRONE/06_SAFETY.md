## WATER QUALITY STANDARDS

| Parameter | Clean | Acceptable | Poor |
|-----------|-------|------------|------|
| pH | 6.5-8.5 | 6.0-9.0 | <6 or >9 |
| Turbidity | < 10 NTU | 10-50 NTU | > 50 NTU |
| Temperature | 10-25°C | 5-35°C | <5 or >35°C |
