# PHI PHASE CAR — Overview

## Vehicle Identity
- **Designation:** PHI-PHASE-CAR-001
- **Type:** Phase-Shifting Automobile
- **Class:** Personal Ground Vehicle (4-wheeled)
- **MSRP:** $2,000

## Core Concept
A four-wheeled automobile capable of shifting its molecular phase state, allowing it to pass through solid barriers (walls, gates, traffic barriers up to 60cm thick). The phase shift is achieved through phi-harmonic phase containment — a more powerful version of the motorcycle's system, scaled for a full vehicle with passengers.

## Key Differentiators
- **Full Phase Shift:** Passes through barriers up to 60cm thick
- **4-Passenger Capacity:** Full family vehicle with phase capability
- **Phi-Harmonic Phase Containment:** Golden-ratio field geometry ensures complete phase coherence across the larger vehicle volume
- **Zero-Emission:** FPB-20 battery power
- **Self-Driving:** AI-assisted navigation with manual override

## Battery
- **Model:** FPB-20 (Field Plasma Battery — 20kWh)
- **Cost:** $2,000

## Primary Applications
- Emergency vehicle bypass (ambulance, fire, rescue)
- Urban transportation through barriers
- Security and military access
- Luxury personal transport
- Autonomous taxi service

## Design Philosophy
The Phase Car scales phase technology from the motorcycle platform to a full passenger vehicle. The challenge is maintaining phase coherence across a 4.5m vehicle with 4 occupants. The phi-harmonic phase containment system solves this by creating a unified field envelope that phases the entire vehicle as a single coherent entity.
