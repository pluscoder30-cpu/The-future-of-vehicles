# PHI PHASE CAR — Structural Design

## Body Architecture
The Phase Car uses a carbon fiber monocoque body designed to maintain structural integrity during phase transitions. The body incorporates phi-spiral reinforcement channels that distribute stress during rapid phase transitions and protect the 4-passenger cabin.

### Body Specifications
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Material | Carbon fiber / aluminum hybrid |
| Weight | 45 kg (body only) |
| Torsional Rigidity | 35,000 Nm/degree |
| Impact Rating | 40g (5-star safety) |
| Phase Transition Fatigue | 200,000 cycles |

### Exterior
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Length | 4,500 mm |
| Width | 1,800 mm |
| Height | 1,400 mm |
| Wheelbase | 2,800 mm |
| Drag Coefficient | Cd 0.22 |
| Ground Clearance | 150 mm |

## Wheel System
### Front Wheels (x2)
- **Type:** Hubless with phi-harmonic bearing
- **Diameter:** 19 inches
- **Tire:** Airless honeycomb polymer (no puncture risk)
- **Brake:** Regenerative + ceramic disc

### Rear Wheels (x2)
- **Type:** Hub motor integrated
- **Diameter:** 19 inches
- **Tire:** Airless honeycomb polymer
- **Drive:** Direct hub motor (no chain/belt)

## Phase Coil Integration
The 12 phase coils are embedded in the body panels:
- 4 coils in front fascia (behind grille)
- 4 coils in side skirts (2 per side)
- 4 coils in rear fascia (behind taillights)
- Each coil: 300mm diameter, 200mm depth
- Total coil weight: 12.0 kg
- Cooling: Liquid-cooled via body-integrated channels

## Passenger Cabin
- **Seating:** 4 passengers (2 front, 2 rear)
- **Interior Volume:** 2.8 m3
- **Headroom:** 950mm (front), 900mm (rear)
- **Legroom:** 1,100mm (front), 900mm (rear)
- **Trunk:** 400L capacity
- **Materials:** White leather, brushed aluminum, phi-trim accents

## Safety Structure
- Front crumple zone: 600mm
- Rear crumple zone: 500mm
- Side impact beams: Boron steel
- Roof crush resistance: 4x vehicle weight
- Phi-harmonic impact dampening: 40% peak G reduction
