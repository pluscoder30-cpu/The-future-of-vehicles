# PHI PHASE CAR — Design Principles

## Phase Containment Theory
A larger vehicle requires a more sophisticated phase system than a motorcycle. The Phase Car uses 12 phase coils arranged in a phi-harmonic dodecahedral pattern, creating a complete phase envelope around the entire vehicle. The golden-ratio geometry ensures that phase windows overlap seamlessly, preventing any part of the vehicle from remaining solid during transit.

## The Containment Equation
```
C_phase = (N_coils / N_min) × (Q × sin(φ × ω × t))²
```
Where:
- C_phase = containment factor (must exceed 1.0 for full transit)
- N_coils = 12 (actual coil count)
- N_min = 8 (minimum for dodecahedral coverage)
- Q = quality factor
- φ = golden ratio
- ω = containment angular frequency

## Core Design Tenets

### 1. Passenger Safety
Unlike the motorcycle, the Phase Car carries passengers who may not understand phase technology. The system is fully automated — passengers experience no sensation during transit.

### 2. Traffic Integration
The car operates normally in traffic, using conventional electric drive. Phase mode is reserved for barrier transit and is only available in designated zones or with explicit rider authorization.

### 3. Autonomous Capability
The Phase Car includes self-driving AI that can navigate to a barrier, assess it, and execute phase transit without rider intervention. Manual override is always available.

### 4. Structural Integrity
The car must maintain structural integrity during phase transitions while carrying passengers. The phi-harmonic containment field ensures the entire vehicle phases as a single coherent entity.

- **Form:** Sleek sedan silhouette with visible phase coil arrays in door panels
- **Color:** Deep obsidian with golden phi-trace patterns
- **Texture:** Smooth graphene-composite body with micro-etched phi spirals
- **Illumination:** Golden glow from phase coils during preparation
- **Interior:** Minimalist, white leather with gold phi accents
