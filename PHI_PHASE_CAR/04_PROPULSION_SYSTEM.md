# PHI PHASE CAR — Propulsion System

## Dual-Mode Propulsion
The Phase Car operates in two distinct modes:

### Mode 1: Normal Driving
- 4 independent hub motors drive all 4 wheels
- Conventional tire contact with road
- Zero emissions, near-silent operation
- Top speed: 180 km/h
- Range: 400 km

### Mode 2: Phase-Shifting
- Phase containment engages, car enters phase state
- Tires lose contact with road surface (floating)
- Car passes through solid barriers
- Top speed (phased): 40 km/h
- Range (phased): 60 minutes / 40 km

## Phase Shift Sequence
1. **Preparation** (3 sec): Coils power up, passengers notified
2. **Barrier Scan** (1 sec): Ultrasonic maps barrier
3. **Synchronization** (0.5 sec): Vehicle speed matched
4. **Phase Engage** (variable): All 12 coils modulate
5. **Transit** (variable): Car passes through barrier
6. **Phase Disengage** (0.5 sec): Coils power down
7. **Recovery** (1 sec): Systems normalize
