- **First drive: no passengers, no phase shift, under 30 km/h.**

## 1. Introduction
The PHI Phase Car is a four-wheeled automobile capable of shifting its molecular phase state to pass through solid barriers. It uses 12 phi-harmonic phase coils in a dodecahedral arrangement to create a complete phase envelope around the vehicle and all 4 passengers.

## 2. Safety Information
**WARNING:** Phase shifting involves temporary reduction of material solidity. Read this manual completely before first drive.

### Required Safety Equipment
- Seatbelts (automatic, always wear)
- No special equipment required for passengers

### Operating Limitations
- Maximum vehicle + cargo + passengers: 565 kg
- Maximum barrier thickness: 60 cm
- Do not phase through walls with live electrical wiring
- Do not phase in crowded areas
- 45-second cooldown between phase shifts
- Minimum age to drive: 18 years

## 3. Getting Started
1. Approach vehicle (auto-unlocks via phone proximity)
2. Enter vehicle (any door)
3. Sit in any seat, fasten seatbelt (automatic tensioning)
4. Press power button on center console
5. AI system performs self-test (90 seconds)
6. Dashboard displays "READY" in green
7. Select driving mode: Eco, Normal, Sport, or Phase
8. Vehicle is ready to drive

## 4. Normal Driving
- Steering: Yoke-style, power-assisted
- Accelerator: Floor pedal, pressure-sensitive
- Brake: Floor pedal, regenerative + ceramic
- Dashboard: Speed, navigation, battery, climate

### Driving Modes
- **Eco:** 50% power, maximum range
- **Normal:** 75% power, balanced
- **Sport:** 100% power, aggressive

## 5. Autonomous Driving
1. Select "Autonomous" mode on dashboard
2. Confirm destination on navigation
3. AI takes full control
4. Driver can override at any time (touch steering or pedals)
5. Phase operations in autonomous mode require passenger consent

## 6. Phase Shift Mode
### Engaging Phase Shift
1. Ensure barrier ahead is within 100m
2. AI scans barrier (automatic)
3. AI confirms transit safe (green indicator)
4. Press phase button (center console, two-hand activation)
5. Dashboard shows "PHASE READY" in gold
6. Vehicle enters phase state (golden glow on coils)
7. Pass through barrier
8. Phase disengages automatically after clearing

### Phase Shift Rules
- 45-second cooldown between shifts
- Barrier must be >10cm and <60cm thick
- Scanner must confirm no live wires or occupants
- All passengers must be seated and belted
- AI must authorize (driver can override, but AI warns)

## 7. Barrier Transit
The ultrasonic scanner pre-maps the barrier. During transit:
- Vehicle floats (tires lose contact)
- Interior lights dim to reduce disorientation
- Time to pass 60cm wall: 0.54 seconds
- Passengers feel no sensation
- Dashboard shows transit progress

## 8. Parking and Charging
1. Come to complete stop
2. Engage park (P button)
3. Power off (hold power button 3 sec)
4. Unbuckle seatbelts
5. Exit vehicle (doors auto-unlock)

### Charging
- **Phi-Harmonic Dock:** 30-minute full charge
- **Standard Outlet:** 4-hour full charge
- **Solar Roof:** Trickle charge when parked outdoors
- **Regenerative:** Recovers 20% during driving

## 10. Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| No phase engage | Battery <30% | Charge battery |
| Phase abort mid-transit | Barrier anomaly | AI finds alternate route |
| Yellow warning | Maintenance due | Schedule service |
| Red warning | Fault detected | Stop, consult error code |
| AI unavailable | Software error | Restart system (hold power 10 sec) |
| Weak phase field | Coil degradation | Service required |

## 11. Emergency Procedures
### Phase Stall in Barrier
1. System attempts automatic recovery (5 tries)
2. If unsuccessful: Triple-tap phase button
3. If still stuck: Hold phase button 5 seconds (forced re-solidify)
4. Post-recovery: Full system check required

### Collision
1. Airbags deploy automatically
2. Engine cuts off
3. Emergency beacon activates
4. Doors auto-unlock
5. Exit vehicle when safe
6. Do not attempt to drive after collision

### Power Failure
1. Phase coils lock out (no phase possible)
2. Drive motors reduce to 40%
3. AI navigates to safe stopping point
4. Contact assistance

### Fire (Extremely Rare)
1. Exit vehicle immediately
2. Move 50m away
3. Call emergency services
4. Do not attempt to extinguish
