# PHI PHASE CAR — Safety Systems

## Safety Philosophy
The Phase Car carries passengers who may be unfamiliar with phase technology. The safety system is designed to protect all occupants with zero required knowledge of phase operations.

## Multi-Layer Safety Architecture

### Layer 1: Pre-Transit Safety
- Multi-frequency ultrasonic barrier scan
- AI barrier composition analysis
- Occupant detection in barrier (impossible — AI refuses transit)
- Driver + passenger authorization required
- Minimum speed and alignment check

### Layer 2: Transit Safety
- Phase window synchronization across all 12 coils
- Real-time barrier re-scan during transit
- Occupant harness auto-tightening
- Cabin pressurization equalization
- Interior lighting dims to reduce disorientation

### Layer 3: Post-Transit Safety
- Phase disengage verification (all sensors confirm solid)
- 3-second stabilization before full control restored
- Passenger comfort check (climate, lighting)
- Post-transit system diagnostic

### Layer 4: Emergency Systems
- Emergency phase abort (instant re-solidification)
- Emergency stop (full braking + phase disengage)
- Airbag system (8 airbags: front, side, curtain, rear)
- Crash detection and emergency services alert

## Occupant Protection
### Front Seat
- 3-point automatic seatbelt with pre-tensioner
- Front airbag (driver + passenger)
- Side torso airbag
- Knee airbag

### Rear Seat
- 3-point automatic seatbelt with pre-tensioner
- Side curtain airbag
- Rear headrest airbag
- Child seat anchors (ISOFIX)

## Barrier Safety
- **Minimum Thickness:** 10cm (thinner barriers risk incomplete transit)
- **Maximum Thickness:** 60cm
- **Composition Detection:** Alerts for metal, reinforced concrete, live wires
- **Live Wire Detection:** Phase aborts if electrical current detected
- **Occupant Detection:** Phase aborts if living beings detected
- **Structural Integrity:** AI refuses transit if barrier may collapse

## Crash Protection
- Airbag system (8 airbags, 30ms response)
- Phi-harmonic impact dampening (40% peak G reduction)
- Automatic engine cutoff within 10ms
- Emergency beacon activation
- GPS location broadcast
- Automatic emergency services notification

## Inspection Protocol
- Pre-drive automated self-test (90 seconds)
- Phase coil integrity check (all 12 coils)
- Barrier scanner calibration
- Harness and airbag verification
- Battery and power system health
- AI system diagnostic
