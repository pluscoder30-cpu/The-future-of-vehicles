# 46 — DRONE BAY

## Overview

The Drone Bay occupies **Deck 28** of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1, repurposed from Fuel & Energy Storage to house and maintain the ship's entire autonomous drone fleet. With 246km × 61.5km = 15,130 km² of floor area, Deck 28 provides space for **400,000 drones** across eight functional classes, plus 10,000 AI command drones, with charging infrastructure, maintenance facilities, drone charging stations, and phi-harmonic launch systems that deploy drones to every deck of the ship.

## Emergency Procedures

### Drone Fire Protocol

1. AI detects fire in drone storage (< 1 second)
2. Affected zone sealed (fire doors close, < 3 seconds)
3. Charged fire drones dispatched (< 5 seconds)
4. Novec 1230 gas suppression activated (< 5 seconds)
5. Unaffected drones retracted to safe zone (< 30 seconds)
6. Battery isolation for affected zone (< 10 seconds)
7. Emergency ventilation activated

### Toxic Drone Contamination Protocol

1. Toxic sensor detects contamination (< 1 second)
2. Affected drone(s) sealed in containment (< 3 seconds)
3. Decontamination shower activated (< 10 seconds)
4. Nearest toxic drones deployed for area assessment
5. Human personnel evacuated from zone
6. Contamination containment verified before re-entry

### Mass Drone Deployment Protocol (Emergency)

1. AI Command activates full fleet alert
2. All drones fully charged (priority charging)
3. All maintenance drones recalled (< 5 minutes)
4. Fleet deployed to emergency locations
5. AI Command coordinates swarm response
6. Continuous monitoring until emergency resolved
