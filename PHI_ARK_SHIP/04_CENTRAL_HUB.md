# 04 — CENTRAL HUB

## Hub Location

The Central Hub is located at the geometric center of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 interior. In folded space coordinates, this is:

```
Length: 123,000m from fore (center of 246km length)
Width: 30,750m from port (center of 61.5km width)
Height: 18,450m from bottom (center of 37km height)
```

The Hub is a sphere of radius 5,000m (5km) centered at these coordinates. It spans from Deck 15 to Deck 19 vertically, and occupies the central 10km × 10km area on those decks.

## Hub Functions

### 1. Coordination Center

The Hub is the nerve center of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1. All ship-wide decisions flow through the Hub.

**Components**:
- **Navigation Control**: Star tracking, course plotting, fold navigation
- **Systems Monitoring**: Real-time status of all 33 decks
- **Communication Hub**: External and internal communication routing
- **Emergency Coordination**: Incident response and resource allocation

**Location**: Zone H1 (north hemisphere of Hub sphere)

### 2. AI Core

The Hub houses the primary AI processing center — the "brain" of the ship.

**Components**:
- **Primary AI**: 100 EFLOPS processing, 100 EB storage
- **Redundant AI nodes**: 10 backup nodes (10 EFLOPS each)
- **Data storage**: All ship data, passenger records, system logs
- **Decision engines**: Real-time optimization for power, life support, navigation

**Location**: Central Core (center of Hub sphere)

### 3. Power Distribution

The Hub is the primary power distribution point for the entire ship.

**Components**:
- **Main bus**: 1,000 GW capacity
- **Distribution transformers**: 10,000 units
- **Emergency backup**: 100 GW battery bank
- **Monitoring**: Real-time power flow visualization

**Location**: Central Core (lower hemisphere)

### 4. Medical Command

The Hub coordinates all medical operations across the ship.

**Components**:
- **Emergency triage**: 10,000-bed emergency hospital
- **Telemedicine center**: Remote consultation for all decks
- **Disease monitoring**: Early warning for pandemics
- **Mental health center**: Psychology and counseling services

**Location**: Zone H2 (south hemisphere of Hub sphere)

### 5. Emergency Response

The Hub coordinates all emergency response operations.

**Components**:
- **Fire command**: Coordinates 500 fire stations
- **Medical command**: Coordinates 200 hospitals
- **Security command**: Coordinates 500 police stations
- **Structural command**: Monitors hull integrity

**Location**: Zone H2 (south hemisphere of Hub sphere)

## Hub Emergency Procedures

### Fold Field Failure

1. **T+0s**: Hub AI detects fold field anomaly
2. **T+0.5s**: Emergency fold field boosters activated
3. **T+1s**: All elevators recalled to nearest deck
4. **T+2s**: Public address: "Emergency. All persons proceed to reinforced zones."
5. **T+5s**: If boosters fail, AI begins emergency compression protocol
6. **T+30s**: Compression begins
7. **T+60s**: Compression complete (if not mitigated)

### Power Failure

1. **T+0s**: Main power bus fails
2. **T+0.1s**: Backup batteries activate (100 GW)
3. **T+1s**: Micro-fusion reactors start (10 GW)
4. **T+5s**: Systems stabilized on backup power
5. **T+60s**: Main power restoration attempted

### Medical Emergency

1. **T+0s**: Medical alert received
2. **T+5s**: Emergency team dispatched
3. **T+30s**: Team arrives at scene
4. **T+60s**: Patient stabilized or transported to Hub hospital
