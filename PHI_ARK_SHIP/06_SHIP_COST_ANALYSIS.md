## Cost Philosophy

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 is built for dirt-cheap by using:
1. **Commodity materials**: Aluminum, copper, barium titanate — not exotic alloys
2. **Community labor**: 8 billion people contributing $350 and 100 hours each
3. **Simple manufacturing**: No precision machining (except resonance cavities)
4. **Scale economics**: 140,000 hull panels = bulk pricing
5. **AI coordination**: No expensive human management overhead

- **$350 USD equivalent** in money or materials
