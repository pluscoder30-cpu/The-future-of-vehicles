## **Deck Number: 21**
## **Purpose: Intensive Food Production for 8 Billion+ People**

---

## **PHILOSOPHY & PURPOSE**

The Agriculture Deck is the INDUSTRIAL FOOD PRODUCTION CENTER of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 — a 246km × 61.5km × 1,118m megastructure containing hydroponic farms, vertical farming towers, mushroom cultivation, algae farms, insect protein farms, and food synthesizer stations. While the Biosphere (Decks 15-20) provides natural food sources, the Agriculture Deck provides INTENSIVE, CONTROLLED, HIGH-YIELD food production that can be precisely calibrated to meet exact nutritional needs.
