## GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 — Folded-Space Interstellar Ark

**Motto**: *"Carrying Earth forward, folded in resonance"*

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 is a city-ship designed to transport 8 billion humans across interstellar distances. It uses folded space technology to create 558,000 km³ of interior volume from a 2,000m × 500m × 300m exterior. The ship is self-sustaining for 1,000+ years with closed-loop life support, phi-harmonic warp drive, and consciousness-preserving stasis.
