# 19 — COMMAND BRIDGE

## Overview

The Command Bridge is the supreme operational center of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1. Located at Deck 33 (topmost deck), it provides unobstructed views through phi-holographic observation windows and houses the ship's primary command and control infrastructure. Every system, every crew member, every one of the 8 billion passengers is ultimately monitored and managed from this space.

**Design Philosophy**: The bridge follows phi-harmonic proportional layout — the central command platform sits at the golden ratio point (61.8% from the aft wall), workstations radiate in Fibonacci-spaced clusters, and all displays use phi-modulated refresh rates for optimal crew cognition.
