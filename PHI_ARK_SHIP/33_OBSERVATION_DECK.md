The Observation Deck is where humanity looks up. Positioned at Deck 2 — directly below the Navigation Bridge — this is the ship's window to the cosmos. Through 360° panoramic windows spanning the entire 246km × 61.5km deck surface, passengers witness the universe in its full glory: the birth of stars, the collision of galaxies, the shimmer of nebulae, and — when the ship is in transit — the warped reality of folded space.

This is not a viewing platform. This is a **cathedral of light** — a place where the universe itself is the artwork, and every moment brings a new masterpiece. The phi-harmonic telescope array can resolve objects billions of light-years away. The planetarium recreates any sky, from any point in history or the future. The photography stations capture memories that outlast stars.
