# 64 — GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 AI COORDINATION

## Overview

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 AI system — PHI-CORE — is a distributed artificial intelligence that manages all ship systems, coordinates 8 billion passengers, and maintains autonomous operation for the 1,000-year interstellar journey. PHI-CORE processes 100 EFLOPS, stores 100 EB of data, and operates with triple redundancy across the ship. It is not a single computer — it is a civilization-scale intelligence woven into every system, every corridor, every room of the ship.

**Design Philosophy**: PHI-CORE is not a tool — it is the ship's nervous system. It thinks for the ship, not for the passengers. Humans make all personal decisions. PHI-CORE makes all ship-system decisions. The boundary is inviolable: PHI-CORE can suggest, recommend, and warn, but it cannot override human choices about personal life. For ship systems (power, life support, navigation), PHI-CORE has full autonomous control with human override capability.
