# 48 — AIRCRAFT BAY

## Overview

The Aircraft Bay occupies the **lower section of Deck 27** of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1, sharing the deck with the Vehicle Bay (upper section). With 246km × 61.5km = 15,130 km² of floor area, Deck 27 provides space for **16,100 aircraft** across four classes — light planes, medium planes, shuttles, and spacecraft — with launch/recovery infrastructure, maintenance facilities, fuel depots, and phi-harmonic flight corridors connecting to the ship's exterior hull and the void of space.

## Emergency Procedures

### Aircraft Fire Protocol

1. AI detects fire via smoke/heat sensors (< 1 second)
2. Affected bay sealed (fire doors close, < 3 seconds)
3. Halon replacement gas (Novec 1230) discharged (< 5 seconds)
4. Adjacent aircraft retracted via taxi system (< 30 seconds)
5. Fire crew dispatched (< 2 minutes)
6. Damage assessment and aircraft recovery

### Crash Landing Protocol

1. AI detects aircraft malfunction (< 1 second)
2. Emergency landing strip activated (lights, arresting)
3. Emergency services pre-positioned (< 30 seconds)
4. Crash/fire/rescue team on standby
5. Medical team on standby
6. Accident investigation initiated

### Mass Evacuation Protocol (Ship)

1. All spacecraft pre-positioned at docking bays
2. All shuttles loaded with evacuees
3. Priority: children → elderly → medical → crew → general
4. Launch sequence: spacecraft → shuttles → medium planes
5. Full ship evacuation: < 30 minutes (1.2M persons)
