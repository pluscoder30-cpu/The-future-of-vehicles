# 12 — STASIS BILL OF MATERIALS

## Cost Philosophy

The stasis system follows the same cost philosophy as the rest of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1:

1. **Commodity materials** — aluminum, copper, barium titanate, phi-harmonic field plasma
2. **Mass production** — 8 billion identical pods = extreme economies of scale
3. **Simple manufacturing** — no precision machining (except resonance cavities)
4. **AI coordination** — no expensive human management overhead
5. **Community labor** — passengers help build their own stasis pods
