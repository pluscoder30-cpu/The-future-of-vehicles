# 03 — DECK LAYOUT MASTER

## Overview

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 has 33 vertical decks, each 1,118m tall. The total interior height is 36,897m (37km). Each deck spans 246km × 61.5km = 15,130 km² of floor area.

---

## Phi-Harmonic Design Principle

> A phi-harmonic design principle: all room-level spaces use 1:φ (1:1.618) proportions where architecturally feasible. Corridor and structural dimensions follow engineering requirements. Hull angles use 137.508° (golden angle).

All residential, commercial, and habitable room dimensions below are specified as **width × depth** with φ-ratio (1:1.618). Areas are calculated as W × D, not W². Example: 50 m² → 8.12m × 6.16m (area = 49.98 m² ≈ 50 m²).
