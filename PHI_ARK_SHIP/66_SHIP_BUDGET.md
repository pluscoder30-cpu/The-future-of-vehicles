# 66 — GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 BUDGET

## Overview

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 costs $959 billion total — $119.88 per person for 8 billion passengers. Every system is costed individually, every dollar is accounted for, and every comparison shows why community-built interstellar travel is the most cost-effective transportation ever conceived. The ship costs less per person than a car, provides more living space than a house, and operates for 1,000 years.

**Cost Philosophy**: The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 achieves its absurdly low cost through three mechanisms:
1. **Commodity materials**: Aluminum, copper, barium titanate — dirt-cheap
2. **Community labor**: 8 billion people building their own ship
3. **Fold material**: 122.99x interior expansion at $606/m2 (cheaper than building outward)
