The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 hull is a phi-proportioned elongated octagon measuring 2,000m x 500m x 300m, constructed from 6 layered materials that provide structural integrity, space folding, radiation shielding, and thermal protection. The hull uses 10-layer folded space material (phi-harmonic copper mesh + barium titanate crystals) that expands interior volume by 122.99x — making a 300m-tall ship feel like 37 km tall inside.

**Design Philosophy**: The hull is the ship's skin — simultaneously structural, functional, and protective. It folds space (phi-harmonic material), blocks radiation (water shielding), absorbs micrometeorite impacts (Whipple shield), and maintains structural integrity (aluminum alloy). Every layer serves multiple purposes. No material is wasted.
