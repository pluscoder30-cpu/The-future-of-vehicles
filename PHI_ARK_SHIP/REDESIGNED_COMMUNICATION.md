## Overview

The redesigned communication system eliminates 51,100 km of cable, 100,000 wireless access points, 44 network switches, 12 dish antennas, and 8.1 billion conventional personal communicators. Every physical component is replaced by the carrier field itself — the same field that maintains folded space now serves as the communication backbone. Personal devices become BaTiO₃ crystal communicators that resonate with the field. Emergency alerts use phi-field modulation to project audio and visual information directly, eliminating all speakers, displays, and holographic projectors.

**Design Philosophy**: The carrier field is the network. BaTiO₃ crystals are the nodes. Resonance is the routing protocol. There are no cables to break, no switches to fail, no antennas to misalign. Communication is as fundamental as gravity — it is a property of the field, not a system bolted onto the ship.
