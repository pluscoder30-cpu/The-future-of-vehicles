### Hull Geometry

The octagon is formed by cutting the corners of a rectangle. The cut angle is 137.508° (the golden angle) rather than the standard 135°.

**Standard octagon**: Corner cut at 135°
**GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 octagon**: Corner cut at 137.508°

The 2.5° difference creates phi-harmonic resonance in the hull material, reducing structural fatigue by approximately 12%.

### Hull Panel Layout

The exterior hull is composed of 5m × 5m panels. Total panel count:

```
Surface area = 3,500,000 m²
Panel area = 25 m²
Total panels = 140,000 panels
```

Each panel is independently manufactured and installed. Panels are bolted together with aluminum alloy bolts and sealed with silicone gaskets.

## Emergency Routes

### Evacuation Philosophy

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 is designed for the "worst case" scenario: complete fold field collapse. In this event, the interior dimensions compress from 246km to 2km in seconds. Every person must reach a "survival pod" within 60 seconds.

### Survival Pods

- Count: 100,000 pods
- Capacity: 80 persons each
- Total capacity: 8,000,000 persons (not enough for 8 billion — see below)
- Distribution: Every 2.5 km grid on every deck

**Critical note**: Survival pods are for crew and essential personnel only (8 million). The 8 billion passengers cannot fit in pods. If the fold field collapses, the only viable outcome is re-establishing the fold field before compression completes.

### Fold Field Emergency Protocol

1. **T+0s**: Fold field failure detected
2. **T+0s to T+5s**: Emergency fold field boosters activate (5-second window)
3. **T+5s to T+30s**: If boosters fail, AI begins emergency compression protocol
4. **T+30s to T+60s**: Compression begins — all persons must be in reinforced zones
5. **T+60s**: Compression complete — interior at exterior dimensions

### Reinforced Zones

Each deck has "Reinforced Zones" — areas where the structural walls can withstand compression:

- Count: 500 per deck
- Capacity: 1,000 persons each
- Total: 16,500 per deck × 33 decks = 544,500 persons
- Distribution: Central hub zone only

**This is still not enough for 8 billion.** The only viable solution is to re-establish the fold field. The emergency protocol is:

1. **Phase 1** (0-5s): Boost fold field
2. **Phase 2** (5-30s): If boost fails, evacuate to reinforced zones (crew only)
3. **Phase 3** (30-60s): AI attempts fold field restart
4. **Phase 4** (60s+): If restart fails, ship is lost

### Emergency Signage

All corridors, boulevards, and rooms have:
- Illuminated exit signs (battery-backed, 72-hour duration)
- Directional arrows pointing to nearest evacuation route
- Deck numbers and zone identifiers
- Emergency procedure cards (multilingual)

### Fire Suppression

- Sprinkler system on every deck
- Fire doors every 500m on all corridors
- Smoke detectors every 100m
- Manual fire extinguishers every 50m
- Fireproof construction materials throughout
