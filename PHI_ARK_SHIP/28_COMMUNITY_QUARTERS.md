## 14. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### 14.1 φ-Community Geometry
All community spaces follow φ-proportions:
- **Pathways:** Width × 1.618 = Length of sight line
- **Seating:** Groups of 5, 8, 13 (Fibonacci)
- **Garden beds:** φ-spiral planting patterns
- **Building facades:** Height:Width = 1:1.618
- **Room proportions:** All following golden ratio
- **Wayfinding:** Signage placed at φ-intervals

### 14.2 Biophilic Design
Every community space includes:
- Living plants (minimum 1 plant per 10m²)
- Natural materials (wood, stone, water)
- Natural light (real or simulated)
- Natural sounds (water, birdsong)
- Views to nature (gardens, biosphere)
- Organic shapes (curves, not just rectangles)

### 14.3 Social Collision Design
Spaces designed to foster spontaneous interaction:
- Wide corridors with seating nooks
- Shared amenities (laundry, mail, coffee)
- Common rooms in every residential block
- "Mixing chambers" at transit nodes
- Community bulletin boards
- Shared gardens and workshops
