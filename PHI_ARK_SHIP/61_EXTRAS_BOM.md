# 61 — EXTRAS BILL OF MATERIALS

## Overview

This Bill of Materials (BOM) covers all "extras" systems across the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1: Frequency Biofield Regenerator (54), Dimensional Beacon Array (55), Tether Launch Bay (56), Phase Loading Dock (57), Universal Translator (58), Abundance Loop Node (59), and Adventure Journey Holodeck (60). It details every component, material, and subsystem required to build, operate, and maintain these additional systems for 8 billion occupants.
