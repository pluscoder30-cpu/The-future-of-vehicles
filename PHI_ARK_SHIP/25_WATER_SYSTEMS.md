## **PHILOSOPHY & PURPOSE**

Water is the LIFEblood of the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 — every drop is tracked, recycled, purified, and redistributed with 99.9% efficiency. The Water Systems are not a single deck but a SHIP-WIDE CIRCULATORY SYSTEM that collects, purifies, distributes, and recycles water continuously. The system operates on a 7-day complete cycle, with water moving through natural (biosphere) and artificial (filtration) purification stages.
