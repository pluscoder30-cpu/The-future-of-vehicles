## Mission Profile

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 carries humanity's entire population (8 billion) across interstellar distances. It is not a exploration vessel — it is a city-ship. The interior volume (558,000 km³) provides more living space than Earth's surface area (510 million km²) distributed across 33 vertical decks.

**Primary Mission**: Transport 8 billion humans from Earth orbit to target star system.
**Secondary Mission**: Serve as self-sustaining habitat during multi-year transit.
**Tertiary Mission**: Establish permanent colony upon arrival.

### Exterior Shape Rationale

The elongated octagon shape is chosen for three reasons:

1. **Structural integrity**: Octagonal cross-section distributes stress evenly across hull panels. No single flat surface bears the full load.

2. **Phi-proportioned angles**: The octagon's internal angles are set to 137.508° (the golden angle) rather than the standard 135°. This creates phi-harmonic resonance in the hull material, reducing fatigue.

3. **Aerodynamic profile**: The elongated form (4:1 length-to-width ratio) minimizes drag during planetary approach. The phi-angles prevent vortex shedding.
