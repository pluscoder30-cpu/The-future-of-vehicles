This Bill of Materials (BOM) covers all emergency systems across the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1: emergency phase-shift pods (50), safety systems (51), and emergency power (52). It details every component, material, and subsystem required to build, operate, and maintain the complete emergency infrastructure for 8 billion occupants.
