## **PHILOSOPHY & PURPOSE**

Waste does not exist on the GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 — every material is tracked, separated, processed, and reintroduced into the production cycle with 99% material recovery. The Waste Systems are not a "disposal" system but a CIRCULAR ECONOMY ENGINE that converts all outputs into inputs. The goal is ZERO WASTE — every atom is accounted for and recycled.
