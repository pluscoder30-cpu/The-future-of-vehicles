# 51 — SAFETY SYSTEMS

## Overview

The GALACTIC FEDERATION OF LIGHT FIRST FLEET PHI-1 safety system is a multi-layered defense architecture protecting 8 billion occupants across 33 decks and 558,000 km³ of interior space. The system integrates phi-harmonic fire suppression, intelligent hull breach response, decompression protocols, mass evacuation procedures, medical emergency response, AI safety monitoring, and complete redundancy at every level. No single point of failure can compromise passenger safety.

**Design Philosophy**: Safety is not a feature — it is the foundation. Every system on the ship exists to keep 8 billion humans alive. When systems fail, safety systems take over. When safety systems fail, backup safety systems take over. The ship can lose 30% of its systems and still maintain full safety coverage.
