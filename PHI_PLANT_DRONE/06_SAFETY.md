### Emergency Procedures

**If drone loses power in flight:**
1. Drone will auto-land (GPS-assisted)
2. Clear area below
3. Do NOT attempt to catch drone

**If drone becomes unstable:**
1. Reduce throttle to minimum
2. Allow drone to land
3. Check for loose components

**If water system leaks:**
1. Shut off pump immediately
2. Land drone
3. Dry all electronics
4. Check for corrosion

**If seed hopper jams:**
1. Stop seed dispenser
2. Land drone
3. Clear jam manually
4. Test before next flight

## STORAGE AND MAINTENANCE

### Storage

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Temperature | 10-30°C |
| Humidity | 30-70% RH |
| Battery | Store at 50% charge |
| Water system | Drain and dry |
| Seeds | Store separately in cool, dry place |

### Maintenance Schedule

| Interval | Task |
|----------|------|
| Every flight | Visual inspection |
| Every 10 flights | Tighten all bolts |
| Every 25 flights | Calibrate sensors |
| Every 50 flights | Replace propellers |
| Every 100 flights | Full inspection |
| Every 6 months | Replace battery |
| After water use | Flush pump and tubing |

## INCIDENT REPORTING

If any safety incident occurs:

1. Stop all operations immediately
2. Document the incident (photo, video, notes)
3. Preserve the drone and all components
4. Report to relevant authorities if required
5. File internal incident report
6. Review and update procedures
