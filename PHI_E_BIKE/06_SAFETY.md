## Electrical Safety

### Battery Hazards
- **Li-Ion batteries store significant energy** — a short circuit can cause fire
- Never expose battery to temperatures above 60°C (140°F)
- Never puncture, crush, or disassemble battery cells
- If battery swells, smells, or smokes — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY** and move to open air
- Keep away from flammable materials during charging
- **Charge only with the supplied 54.6V Li-Ion charger** — never use other chargers
- Charge in a well-ventilated area, not on beds or couches
- Unplug charger when fully charged
- Never charge battery overnight or unattended

### High Voltage
- The battery operates at **48V DC** — this can cause burns or cardiac arrest
- Never touch battery terminals with bare hands
- Always disconnect battery before working on electronics
- Use insulated tools when working near live circuits
- Wear rubber-soled shoes when operating the e-bike
- If you feel a tingle or shock — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY**

### Motor Heat
- Hub motor can reach temperatures up to 80°C during heavy use
- Never touch motor immediately after riding
- Allow 30 minutes cool-down before handling
- If you smell burning plastic — **STOP USE IMMEDIATELY**

## Mechanical Safety

### Riding Risks
- The e-bike is **heavier than a regular bicycle** (22 kg vs 14 kg)
- This affects handling, braking, and balance
- **Practice riding at low speed first** before using full power
- The motor adds significant acceleration — be prepared
- Keep both hands on handlebars at all times
- Never carry passengers (single-rider only)

### Braking
- E-bikes require **longer stopping distances** than regular bikes
- The motor adds weight and momentum
- **Always brake earlier** than you would on a regular bike
- Use both brakes simultaneously (front 70%, rear 30%)
- Avoid sudden braking on wet or slippery surfaces
- Check brakes before every ride

### Speed
- Maximum speed: 32 km/h (20 mph) — this is **twice the speed** of most cyclists
- **Always ride at a speed appropriate for conditions**
- Slow down in traffic, on curves, near pedestrians
- The motor provides strong acceleration — be ready
- Never exceed local speed limits

### Weight Capacity
- Maximum rider weight: 120 kg (265 lbs)
- This includes rider + clothes + cargo
- Exceeding weight limit reduces performance and safety
- Battery drains faster with heavy loads
- Braking distance increases with weight

## Emergency Procedures

### E-Bike Malfunction
1. **Immediately stop pedaling** and apply brakes gently
2. Coast to a safe stop
3. Turn off power switch
4. If motor runs away (stuck on), use brake cut-off levers
5. If brakes fail, use emergency stop (lean back, drag feet)
6. Move away from traffic

### Battery Emergency
1. If battery smokes or catches fire: **DO NOT USE WATER**
2. Move away from the e-bike
3. Use a Class D fire extinguisher (metal fire) if available
4. Call emergency services (911)
5. If skin contact with battery acid, flush with water for 15 minutes

### Crash Response
1. If you crash, try to protect your head
2. Assess for injuries before standing
3. If you suspect a fracture, do not move the injured area
4. Call for help if needed
5. Move to safety if possible

## Maintenance Safety

### Before Each Ride
- [ ] Check battery charge level (should be >20%)
- [ ] Check brakes work (test both levers)
- [ ] Check tires are inflated (40 PSI)
- [ ] Check all bolts are tight
- [ ] Check motor wire is secure
- [ ] Test throttle and pedal assist

### Weekly Maintenance
- [ ] Clean frame and components
- [ ] Check all electrical connections
- [ ] Test battery balance with multimeter
- [ ] Check brake pad wear
- [ ] Lubricate chain
- [ ] Check spoke tension

### Monthly Maintenance
- [ ] Full battery charge cycle (discharge to 20%, charge to 100%)
- [ ] Check all solder joints for cracks
- [ ] Inspect frame for cracks or damage
- [ ] Test all sensors
- [ ] Check motor bearings (should spin freely)

- **The operator assumes all risk and liability**

- Do not operate under the influence of alcohol or drugs
