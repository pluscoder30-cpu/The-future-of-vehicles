# PHI_E_BIKE — Regulatory Status

## Legal Classification

The phi-harmonic e-bike is classified as a **Class 1 pedal-assist electric bicycle** in most jurisdictions. Here's how it fits into existing laws:

### Federal Classification (United States)

**1. Class 1 E-Bike (Pedal-Assist)**
- Motor activates only when pedaling
- Maximum speed: 20 mph (32 km/h) with motor assist
- Motor power: 500W (within 750W limit for Class 1)
- **Legal on most bike paths, lanes, and roads**

**2. Not a "Motor Vehicle"**
- Under federal law, e-bikes under 750W and 20 mph are **not motor vehicles**
- No registration, insurance, or driver's license required
- No EPA emissions standards apply
- No NHTSA standards apply

**3. State-Level Classification**
Most states have adopted the 3-class e-bike system:

| Class | Description | Max Speed | Our Bike |
|-------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| Class 1 | Pedal-assist only | 20 mph (32 km/h) | ✅ **THIS** |
| Class 2 | Throttle-on-demand | 20 mph (32 km/h) | Can limit to this |
| Class 3 | Speed pedelec | 28 mph (45 km/h) | Can unlock to this |

## Why It's Legal

### 1. Meets Class 1 Requirements
- Pedal-assist only (no throttle above 20 mph)
- 500W motor (under 750W limit)
- 32 km/h max speed (under 32 km/h limit)
- **Fully compliant with Class 1 e-bike laws**

### 2. No Registration Required
- Most states don't require registration for Class 1 e-bikes
- No license plate needed
- No VIN number needed
- Just ride it like a bicycle

### 3. No Insurance Required
- Most states don't require insurance for e-bikes
- Check local laws — some cities may require it
- Homeowner's insurance may cover e-bike liability

### 4. No Special Parking
- Can park at any bicycle rack
- Can bring on public transit (most systems)
- Can store in apartment buildings (most allow bikes)

## How to Stay Legal

### Do's
✅ Ride in bike lanes when available
✅ Follow all traffic laws (same as bicycle)
✅ Wear a helmet (required under 16-18 in most places)
✅ Use lights at night (front white, rear red)
✅ Yield to pedestrians
✅ Signal turns and stops

### Don'ts
❌ Don't ride on sidewalks in business districts (most cities prohibit)
❌ Don't ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs
❌ Don't modify to exceed speed limits
❌ Don't carry passengers (single-rider only)
❌ Don't ride in pedestrian-only zones
❌ Don't block bike lanes when parked

## Phi-Harmonic Motor: Legal Nuance

The phi-harmonic motor arrangement is **functionally identical** to a standard hub motor for legal purposes:

- Same power rating (500W)
- Same speed limit (32 km/h)
- Same pedal-assist operation
- Same classification (Class 1 e-bike)

The phi-harmonic geometry is an **internal design improvement** — it doesn't change the legal classification. It's like using better bearings in a bicycle — still just a bicycle.
