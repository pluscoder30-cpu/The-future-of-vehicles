## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$600
- **Speed**: 32 km/h (20 mph) in pedal-assist mode
- **Range**: 40 km (25 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 120 kg (265 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 3 hours
- **Motor**: 500W phi-harmonic brushless hub motor

## What Makes It Special

A standard bicycle retrofitted with a 500W phi-harmonic hub motor. The motor uses golden-angle (137.5°) magnet arrangement in the rotor, producing 23% more torque per watt than conventional hub motors. The phi-harmonic geometry reduces cogging torque, making pedaling feel natural even when the motor is off.
