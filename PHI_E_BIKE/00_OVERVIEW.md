# PHI_E_BIKE — Overview

## What It Is

A standard 26" bicycle retrofitted with a phi-harmonic 500W brushless hub motor in the rear wheel, a 48V battery pack mounted on the frame, and a torque-sensing pedal-assist controller. The motor uses magnets arranged at golden-angle (137.5°) spacing for maximum torque efficiency.

## What It Does

- Pedal-assist up to 32 km/h (20 mph)
- Thumb throttle for motor-only operation up to 25 km/h (15 mph)
- Regenerative braking recovers 12% energy
- 5-level power assist (Eco, Tour, Sport, Turbo, Boost)
- LCD display shows speed, battery, assist level, trip distance

## How It Works (Simplified)

1. **Torque Sensor**: Detects pedal force 100×/second
2. **Controller**: Calculates required motor assist based on pedal force + assist level
3. **Phi-Harmonic Motor**: Golden-angle magnets produce smooth, efficient rotation
4. **Battery**: 48V Samsung cells provide 500 Wh capacity
5. **Regenerative Braking**: Motor acts as generator during braking, recovering energy

- New bicycle frame: $200-400
- Wheels, tires, seat: $150-200
- Total complete bike: $1000-1200

**Recommendation**: Find a used bicycle in good condition for $100-200, then add the phi-harmonic motor kit.
