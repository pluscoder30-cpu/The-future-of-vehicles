## 3. Temporal Cocoon

### 3.1 Purpose

The temporal cocoon is a protective enclosure that deploys around each passenger during temporal fold transit. It provides:
- Structural support during metric perturbation
- Temporal fold radiation shielding
- Life support during transit
- Vital sign monitoring

### 3.2 Deployment Sequence

```
Temporal cocoon deployment:
  Time -8 sec: Pre-deploy check (cabin pressure, passenger position)
  Time -5 sec: Cocoon shells extend from seat sides
  Time -2 sec: Cocoon shells close around passenger
  Time 0 sec: Temporal fold transit begins (cocoon fully sealed)
  Time +1.2 sec: Temporal fold transit complete
  Time +2 sec: Cocoon shells retract
  Time +5 sec: Cocoon fully retracted
```

### 3.3 Cocoon Specifications

| Internal volume | 0.35 m³ |

| Retraction time | 3 seconds |
| Temporal fold radiation shielding | > 92% attenuation |

| Emergency supply | Water (300 mL), food (1 snack), medical kit |

### 3.4 Temporal Fold Radiation Protection

The temporal cocoon protects passengers from temporal fold radiation:

| Radiation type | Source | Cocoon shielding | Exposure limit |
|----------------|--------|------------------|----------------|
| X-rays | Temporal fold formation | Lead lining (0.8 mm) | < 0.05 mSv per fold |
| Gamma rays | Temporal fold collapse | Lead lining (0.8 mm) | < 0.02 mSv per fold |
| Neutrons | Temporal fold energy | Polyethylene (12 mm) | < 0.005 mSv per fold |
| UV radiation | Temporal fold plasma | CFRP shell (3 mm) | < 0.0005 mSv per fold |
| **Total** | — | — | **< 0.0755 mSv per fold** |

Annual exposure limit: 20 mSv. Maximum folds per year: 265.

### 3.5 Passenger Monitoring During Temporal Fold

The temporal cocoon monitors passenger vital signs throughout the temporal fold:

```
Monitoring during temporal fold:
  Heart rate: 60-100 bpm (normal range)
  Blood oxygen: 95-100% (normal range)
  Respiration: 12-20 breaths/min (normal range)
  Body temperature: 36.1-37.2°C (normal range)
  Acceleration: < 1.2g (temporal fold limit)
  Consciousness: EEG (optional, research mode)
```

If any vital sign goes out of range:
1. Alert crew (audio + visual)
2. Provide medical intervention (automatic)
3. If necessary, trigger temporal fold abort (passenger safety priority)

## 5. Passenger Briefing

### 5.1 Pre-Fold Briefing

Before each temporal fold, passengers receive a briefing:

```
Pre-fold briefing:
  1. Temporal fold sequence explanation (45 sec)
  2. Temporal cocoon deployment (20 sec)
  3. Safety harness check (15 sec)
  4. Communication check (10 sec)
  5. Emergency procedure review (45 sec)
  6. Causal consistency explanation (30 sec)
  7. Questions (45 sec)
  Total: 3.5 minutes
```

### 5.2 Emergency Procedures

Passengers are briefed on:
1. Temporal fold abort procedure (automatic, no passenger action required)
2. Emergency evacuation (manual, crew-directed)
3. Emergency communication (intercom, visual signals)
4. Medical emergency (crew first aid, automatic cocoon response)
5. Causal consistency (events on the fold are self-consistent)
