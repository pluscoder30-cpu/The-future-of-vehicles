# PHI_TIME_SHUTTLE — Time-Folding Temporal Spacecraft

## Classification
- **Class**: PHI Time Shuttle (TMS-1)
- **Type**: Phi-harmonic temporal folding vehicle
- **Manufacturer**: PHI Physics Division
- **Status**: Prototype — flight-tested in controlled temporal laboratory conditions

## Summary

The PHI_TIME_SHUTTLE is a time-folding spacecraft that manipulates the temporal dimension using phi-harmonic standing waves. Unlike the teleport shuttle (which folds space) and the FTL shuttle (which warps spacetime), the time shuttle folds the time dimension itself, allowing the vehicle to shift backward or forward in time by up to 24 hours.

The shuttle does not "travel through time" in the traditional sense — it creates a temporal fold that connects two points in the time dimension separated by up to 24 hours. The vehicle traverses the temporal fold in the same way the teleport shuttle traverses a spatial fold: the temporal interval between the two points is collapsed to zero.

## Operating Principle

Time folding is achieved through **temporal dimension manipulation** — a process where the local time metric is temporarily reconfigured so that two points in time separated by up to 24 hours become coincident in the folded manifold. This is not time travel in the sense of moving through a time dimension — it is a genuine geometric manipulation of the temporal metric.

The process uses phi-harmonic resonance to create and sustain a temporal fold node. The golden ratio φ = 1.6180339887... is the eigenvalue of the fold transformation matrix, ensuring recursive self-similarity across temporal scales and preventing metric degradation during the fold.
