## How It Works

The shuttle folds the **temporal dimension** using phi-harmonic standing waves. The temporal coils create two temporal fold nodes — one at the vehicle's current time, one at the target time — connected by a temporal fold bridge where the time metric is collapsed to zero interval. The vehicle traverses the temporal fold bridge in 1.2 seconds, arriving at the target time (up to 24 hours in the past or future).
