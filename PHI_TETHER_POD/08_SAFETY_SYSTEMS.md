# PHI TETHER POD — Safety Systems

## Safety Philosophy
The Tether Pod is designed with the assumption that everything will fail. Every critical system has at least two independent backups, and the default state on any failure is safe descent.

## Multi-Layer Safety Architecture

### Layer 1: Phi-Harmonic Redundancy
- 3 independent tether excitation channels
- Any single channel can maintain safe suspension
- Any two channels provide full performance
- All three required for maximum lift

### Layer 2: Mechanical Failsafe
- Tether base strength: 15 kN (supports 150% rated load without phi-harmonic amplification)
- Anchor system rated for 10x maximum load
- Structural shell rated for 50g impact

### Layer 3: Controlled Descent
- Auto-descent on power failure (0.5 m/s)
- Manual descent control at all times
- Parachute backup deploys at >3 m/s descent rate

### Layer 4: Emergency Equipment
- Built-in First Aid kit (under seat)
- Emergency whistle and signal mirror
- 433 MHz personal locator beacon
- Solar-powered emergency light

## Harness System
- 5-point phi-lock safety harness
- Single-hand release for emergency exit
- Load-rated to 25 kN per attachment point
- Anti-entanglement design

## Environmental Protection
- IP67 waterproofing (survives submersion to 1m for 30 minutes)
- UV-resistant shell (20+ year outdoor rating)
- Operating range: -40C to +85C
- Lightning protection: Faraday cage shell

## Inspection Protocol
- Pre-flight visual inspection (2 minutes)
- Automated self-test on power-up (30 seconds)
- Tether integrity check via phi-harmonic reflection
- Anchor security verification
