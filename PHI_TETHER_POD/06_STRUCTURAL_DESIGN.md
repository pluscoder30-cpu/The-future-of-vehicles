# PHI TETHER POD — Structural Design

## Pod Structure
The pod is a single-piece graphene-composite shell molded in an organic teardrop form that follows the natural catenary curve of a suspended vessel.

### Shell Specifications
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Material | Graphene-reinforced polymer composite |
| Thickness | 3.2mm (single wall) |
| Weight | 4.8 kg |
| Impact Rating | 50g (5× automotive standard) |
| Operating Temp | -40°C to +85°C |
| UV Resistance | 20+ year outdoor exposure |
| Water Rating | IP67 (submersible 1m, 30 min) |

### Interior
- **Seating:** Ergonomic hammock-style suspension seat
- **Padding:** Memory foam with graphene thermal regulation
- **Harness:** 5-point phi-lock safety harness
- **Space:** 0.8m × 0.6m × 0.9m occupant volume
- **Storage:** 5L integrated cargo pocket

## Tether Anchor Point
The tether connects to the pod at the top apex through a phi-harmonic coupling joint:
- **Material:** Titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V)
- **Load Rating:** 2,000 kN (10× amplified load)
- **Articulation:** 360° rotation, ±45° tilt
- **Damping:** Integrated vibration absorber (phi-tuned)

## Foldable Architecture
The pod collapses for transport:
1. Tether spool retracts (auto-wind)
2. Pod shell folds along pre-scored phi-spiral lines
3. Seat collapses flat
4. Control unit detaches and clips to exterior
5. Total packed dimensions: 0.4m × 0.3m × 0.2m
6. Total weight: 8.2 kg (including battery and tether)

## Phi-Spiral Structural Geometry
The pod shell incorporates phi-spiral ribbing that serves dual purposes:
1. **Structural:** Distributes impact loads along the spiral, preventing stress concentration
2. **Aesthetic:** Visible golden-ratio spirals on the interior surface
3. **Functional:** Channels for wiring, ventilation, and field emitter conduits
