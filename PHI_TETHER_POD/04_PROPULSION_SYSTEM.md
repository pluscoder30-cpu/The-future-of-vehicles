# PHI TETHER POD — Propulsion & Suspension System

## Suspension Principle
The Tether Pod does not "fly" in the traditional sense. It suspends via a phi-harmonic tether that creates a standing wave in the local gravitational field gradient. The tether, excited at the golden-ratio frequency, generates a coherent field that partially cancels the gravitational potential along its length, reducing the effective weight of the pod and occupant.

## Phi-Harmonic Field Coupling
The tether contains embedded piezoelectric phi-field emitters at 1-meter intervals. When excited, these emitters create a coherent golden-ratio standing wave that propagates along the tether. The field gradient produces a lifting force:

```
F_lift = m × g × (1 - e^(-Q × sin²(φ × ω × t)))
```

At Q=100, this achieves ~92% gravitational cancellation at the pod, reducing effective weight to 8% of mass.
