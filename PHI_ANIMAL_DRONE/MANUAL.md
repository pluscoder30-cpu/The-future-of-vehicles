# PHI ANIMAL DRONE — Kid-Friendly Instruction Manual

## What Is the PHI Animal Drone?

The PHI Animal Drone is a quiet flying robot that helps take care of wild animals! It can watch animals with a special heat camera, give them food, and play calming sounds to help them feel safe. It costs less than $200 to build!

### How Does It Help Animals?

1. **WATCHES** — Thermal camera sees animals even at night
2. **FEEDS** — Drops food for hungry animals
3. **CALMS** — Plays sounds that help animals feel safe
