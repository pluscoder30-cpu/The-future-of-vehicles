# PHI ANIMAL DRONE — WILDLIFE PROTOCOLS

## Animal Care Guidelines

---

## OBSERVATION PROTOCOL

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Approach from downwind |
| 2 | Hover at 10m distance |
| 3 | Activate thermal camera |
| 4 | Record animal behavior |
| 5 | Monitor vital signs (thermal) |
| 6 | Log observations |

---

## FEEDING PROTOCOL

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Identify animal species |
| 2 | Select appropriate food |
| 3 | Load food dispenser |
| 4 | Hover 5m above feeding area |
| 5 | Dispense food (5g portions) |
| 6 | Monitor animal response |

---

## MEDICAL CARE PROTOCOL

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Identify injured animal |
| 2 | Assess severity (thermal + visual) |
| 3 | If minor: apply calming frequencies |
| 4 | If major: contact wildlife rehabilitation |
| 5 | Do NOT attempt to handle animal |
| 6 | Monitor until help arrives |

---

## SEASONAL CONSIDERATIONS

| Season | Activity | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Spring | Nesting observation | Do not approach nests |
| Summer | Feeding | Provide water in hot weather |
| Fall | Migration tracking | Observe from distance |
| Winter | Emergency feeding | Supplement natural food |
