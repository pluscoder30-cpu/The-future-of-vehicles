## WHAT IS THE PHI ANIMAL DRONE?

The PHI Animal Drone observes, feeds, and provides medical care to wild animals using phi-harmonic calming frequencies. It features whisper-quiet operation (<45dB), thermal imaging for night observation, and a food/medication delivery system.
