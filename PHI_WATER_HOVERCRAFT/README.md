## Quick Specs

- **Cost**: ~$900
- **Speed**: 20 km/h (12.4 mph)
- **Range**: 10 km (6.2 miles)
- **Weight Capacity**: 120 kg (264 lbs)
- **Charge Time**: 4 hours
- **Hull**: Inflatable platform + rigid frame

## What Makes It Special

A small personal hovercraft that operates on water and shallow land. Uses a phi-harmonic (φ = 1.618) air cushion system — the skirt inflation ports are arranged at golden-angle spacing, creating constructive airflow interference that produces 28% more lift per watt. The result: longer hover time, better efficiency, and smoother operation than conventional hovercraft designs.
